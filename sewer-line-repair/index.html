Signs your sewer line needs repair

  • Backups in the lowest drain in the house, like a basement shower or floor drain
  • Several drains slow or gurgling at the same time
  • Main line clogs that come back after cleaning
  • Sewage odor inside or outside the house
  • Soggy, sunken or unusually green patches along the sewer path
  • Rodent or insect activity near the foundation or cleanout

What causes side sewer problems

  • Tree roots entering joints and cracks, especially in older clay or concrete pipe
  • Bellies or sags where settled pipe holds water and waste
  • Offset or separated joints from shifting soil
  • Broken or collapsed pipe from age, roots or heavy loads above
  • Corroded cast iron under or near the house
  • Grease and debris narrowing the line

Why a sewer camera inspection comes first

A sewer camera inspection runs a waterproof camera through the line from a cleanout or roof vent, showing exactly what's wrong and how far from the house it is. It tells you whether drain cleaning will be enough or whether the pipe needs repair, and it lets the plumber mark the problem spot from the surface. Ask for a copy of the video.

A sewer scope is also worth considering before buying an older home in Burien. Side sewer repairs can be expensive, and a pre-purchase inspection shows the line's condition before you close.

Sewer line repair FAQs

Who is responsible for the side sewer in Burien?

Generally the property owner is responsible for the side sewer from the house to the connection with the public sewer main. The sewer district maintains the main itself. Your district can confirm exactly where its responsibility starts for your address, which matters when planning repairs or replacement.

Is trenchless sewer repair better than digging?

Trenchless repair disturbs far less of your yard, driveway and landscaping, which is a big advantage on sloped Burien lots. However, it isn't suitable for every line. Severe sags, fully collapsed pipe and some soil conditions still need excavation. A camera inspection helps decide which method fits.

Can tree roots be removed without replacing the pipe?

Yes. Roots can be cut out with a machine auger or hydro jetter, which restores flow quickly. However, roots usually grow back through the same joints within months to a few years. Lining the pipe or replacing the damaged section with jointless pipe is the long-term fix.

Should I get a sewer scope before buying a house in Burien?

For older homes, a sewer scope is a smart step. It shows roots, cracks, sags and offset joints before you close. Side sewer repairs can be expensive, so knowing the line's condition can affect your offer, your repair requests, or your decision to buy the home.

How long does side sewer replacement take?

Many residential side sewer replacements take one to a few days, depending on the method, the length and depth of the line, access, and inspection scheduling. Trenchless work is often faster than open-trench replacement. The plumber should give you a timeline with the written estimate.
