What does professional drain cleaning include?

A plumber clears the blockage, restores full flow, and checks that the problem won't come straight back. Depending on the drain, that can mean anything from a quick snake through a bathroom sink to cleaning a full main sewer line from an outdoor cleanout. Drain cleaning services in Burien typically include:

  • Kitchen sink drain cleaning for grease, food and soap buildup
  • Bathroom sink, tub and shower drains clogged with hair and soap scum
  • Toilet clogs, including objects and "flushable" wipes
  • Laundry and floor drains in garages, utility rooms and basements
  • Main sewer line cleaning when several fixtures back up at once
  • Hydro jetting to scour grease, sludge and scale from pipe walls
  • Sewer camera inspection to see roots, breaks or sags

Signs you need drain cleaning

  • Water pools in the sink, tub or shower and drains slowly
  • Gurgling or bubbling sounds from drains or toilets
  • Sewage or musty odors coming from drains
  • The same drain clogs again within a few weeks
  • Water comes up in the tub when you flush the toilet
  • Water backs up into the lowest drain in the house

One drain or the main line?

If one fixture is slow and everything else drains normally, the clog is usually in that fixture's branch line, which is a quick fix.

If several drains are slow at once, toilets gurgle when a sink drains, or water rises in the tub when you flush, the blockage is probably in the main line or side sewer. That's a bigger job and may call for a camera inspection. Mention which one it is when you call so the plumber brings the right equipment.

Why drains clog in Burien homes

Kitchen clogs usually come from grease, fats and food scraps that cool and harden in the pipe. Bathroom clogs are mostly hair and soap residue. Wipes, paper towels and feminine products are common causes of toilet and main line clogs, even when the packaging says "flushable."

In Burien's older neighborhoods, main line clogs often have a structural cause. Clay and concrete side sewers have joints that tree roots can work into, and older cast-iron drain pipe builds up scale inside that narrows the pipe over time. Long downhill side sewers on sloped lots, common in Seahurst and Three Tree Point, can develop low spots where waste collects. Wooded areas like Normandy Park see frequent root-related clogs. If roots or a damaged pipe are behind the problem, cleaning only buys time, and sewer line repair is the longer-term fix.

When to try DIY and when to call a plumber

You can try a cup plunger on sinks and tubs, a flange plunger on toilets, and a plastic drain tool to pull hair from shower drains. If you're comfortable, you can also clean a sink's P-trap with a bucket underneath.

Skip chemical drain cleaners, especially in older homes. They often don't clear the clog, they can damage older pipe and fittings, and they leave caustic water in the drain for whoever comes next. Call a plumber if plunging doesn't work, more than one drain is affected, the clog keeps coming back, or sewage is backing up. A backup into the house should be treated as emergency plumbing.

Drain cleaning FAQs

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes in Burien homes?

It depends on the pipe's condition. Hydro jetting is very effective on grease and scale, but cracked or badly deteriorated clay or cast-iron pipe can be damaged by high pressure. Many plumbers run a sewer camera first on older Burien lines to decide whether jetting is safe.

How often should a main sewer line be cleaned?

Most homes don't need a fixed schedule. If roots or buildup cause clogs every year or more often, a plumber may suggest regular cleaning. A camera inspection will show whether a repair, such as lining or replacing a section, would solve the problem for good instead of repeated cleaning.

Are chemical drain cleaners safe to use?

They are not recommended, especially in older homes. Chemical cleaners often fail on solid clogs, can damage older pipes and fittings, and leave caustic water sitting in the drain. That water is dangerous for whoever clears the clog next. A plunger or drain snake is a safer first step.

What is a sewer cleanout and do I have one?

A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives direct access to your main drain line, usually outside near the foundation or in the crawl space. It lets a plumber clear the main line without removing a toilet. Many older Burien homes lack one, and adding one makes future service easier.

Why does my kitchen sink keep clogging?

Grease, oil and food scraps cool and harden inside the pipe, then catch more debris every time you run water. A snake opens a path but leaves buildup on the walls. Hydro jetting removes that buildup, and keeping grease out of the sink helps prevent the clog from coming back.
