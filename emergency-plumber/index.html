What does an emergency plumber do?

An emergency plumber's first job is to make the situation safe: stop the water, clear the backup, or isolate the failed fixture so the damage stops growing. Once that's done, the plumber diagnoses the cause and explains the permanent repair. Sometimes both happen in the same visit. Other times, a burst pipe is capped the same day and the section is replaced once walls or ceilings are opened and dried.

Emergency plumbing in Burien typically covers:

  • Burst and frozen pipes in crawl spaces, garages, attics and exterior walls
  • Major leaks from supply lines, fittings, valves and toilet or washing machine hoses
  • Sewer and main line backups coming up through tubs, showers, floor drains or toilets
  • Overflowing toilets that won't stop running, especially in single-bathroom homes
  • Water heater failures where the tank is leaking or the relief valve is discharging
  • Failed main shutoff valves that won't close when you need them to

Signs you need an emergency plumber now

  • Water is spraying, pouring or coming through a ceiling, wall or light fixture
  • Sewage is coming up through a drain anywhere in the house
  • You can't stop water from running, even after closing the fixture's shutoff
  • The water heater tank is leaking from the body or pooling water quickly
  • You have no water at all while neighbors have normal service
  • Your only toilet is overflowing or completely unusable

Problems that can usually wait

Some problems are frustrating but not urgent. Booking a regular appointment for these usually costs less than an after-hours call:

  • A dripping faucet or a toilet that runs between flushes
  • One slow drain while every other drain works normally
  • Lukewarm water when you still have some hot water
  • A small, steady drip you can catch in a bucket and shut off at the fixture

For these, see drain cleaning or water heater repair and schedule a normal visit.

What to do before the emergency plumber arrives

The single most useful thing you can do is stop the water. These steps limit damage and help the plumber work faster:

  1. Shut off the main water valve. In most Burien homes it's where the water line enters the house, often in the crawl space, garage or a utility closet near the water heater. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If it won't close, the water meter box near the street usually has a shutoff, though it may need a meter key.
  2. Turn off the water heater. Set a gas unit to "pilot" or "off," or switch off the breaker for an electric unit.
  3. Open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain remaining pressure out of the pipes.
  4. Stay clear of electrical hazards. Don't walk through standing water near outlets, cords or the electrical panel.
  5. Stop using every drain if sewage is backing up. Each flush or load of laundry adds to the backup.
  6. Document the damage with photos and video before cleanup, for your insurance claim.

If you smell gas, leave the building right away and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Don't use light switches or phones near the smell. A plumber is not the first call for a suspected gas leak.

When to call a professional instead of DIY

You can safely shut off water, turn off the water heater, and plunge a single clogged toilet. Call a plumber if water won't stop at the shutoff, sewage is involved, the problem is inside a wall or ceiling, or the water heater is leaking. Avoid thawing frozen pipes with an open flame, and don't use chemical drain cleaners on a backed-up main line.

Emergency plumbing FAQs

Where is the main water shutoff in a Burien house?

In most Burien homes the main shutoff is where the water line enters the house, often in the crawl space, garage or a utility closet near the water heater. There is also a shutoff at the water meter in a box near the street, which may need a meter key to turn.

Can I get an emergency plumber in Burien at night?

Yes, night and weekend calls are connected when a plumber offering urgent service is available. Availability varies by time and job type, and after-hours rates are usually higher. Ask for an arrival window and any after-hours fee when you are connected, and shut off the water while you wait.

Should I call my insurance company or a plumber first?

Stop the water first, then call a plumber to make the situation safe. Take photos and video of the damage before cleanup, and keep the plumber's invoice describing the cause. Contact your insurer as soon as practical. Your policy determines whether the damage and the repair are covered.

Is a sewer backup a plumbing emergency?

Yes. Sewage is a health hazard, and the backup gets worse every time water goes down any drain in the house. Stop using sinks, toilets, the dishwasher and washing machine, keep people and pets away from the water, and call for an emergency plumber right away.

How do I prevent frozen pipes in Burien?

Before a cold snap, disconnect garden hoses, cover outdoor faucets, insulate pipes in crawl spaces and garages, and close crawl space vents. When temperatures drop well below freezing, let a faucet on an exterior wall drip slowly. If a pipe freezes, never thaw it with an open flame.
