How to Find Your Main Water Shutoff Valve

Every adult in the house should know where the main water shutoff is. Here is where to look in a typical Burien home and how to test it.

Emergencies and Leaks · November 2, 2026 · 4 min read

Call (833) 710-2588

Your main water shutoff valve controls all the water coming into the house. In most Burien homes it sits where the water line enters the building: in the crawl space, the garage, a utility closet or near the water heater. There is also a curb valve in the meter box near the street. Find it and test it now, before a burst pipe or failed hose floods the floor.

When a pipe breaks, every minute counts. Many homeowners lose time searching under the house with a flashlight while water spreads. A two minute check today saves that panic later. It also tells you if the valve is stuck, which is common in older homes where the valve has not moved in decades.

Where is the main shutoff in a Burien home?

The location depends on the age and layout of the house.

Home typeMost likely location
Older home with a crawl spaceUnder the house, on the line from the front yard, near the foundation wall
Home with a basementOn the front wall of the basement where the pipe comes through
Home with a garageOn the garage wall nearest the street, or near the water heater
Newer home or remodelIn a utility closet, laundry room or near the water heater
Condo or townhomeIn a utility closet, under a sink, or a shared valve managed by the HOA

Start by finding your water meter. In Burien it is usually in a box near the street or sidewalk. The water line runs from the meter toward the house in a fairly straight line. Look on the inside of the house along that path.

The two shutoff valves you have

  • The house shutoff. This is the valve you control. It is inside or right outside the house. Use it in an emergency.
  • The meter or curb valve. This sits in the meter box. It often needs a special meter key, and the water district technically owns the meter side. Burien homes are served by Highline Water District or King County Water Districts 20, 49 or 125, depending on the address. Use this valve only if the house valve fails, and call your district if you are unsure.

How to close the valve

  • Gate valve (round wheel handle). Turn it clockwise until it stops. It can take many turns. Do not force a stuck wheel, because old gate valves can break inside.
  • Ball valve (lever handle). Turn the lever a quarter turn so it sits across the pipe. Parallel means open. Across means closed.

After you close it, open a faucet on the lowest floor to confirm the water stops.

How to test your shutoff valve

Test it once a year. Close the valve, open a faucet and make sure the flow stops within a minute. Then open the valve slowly. If the wheel will not turn, the water does not stop, or the valve drips at the stem, it needs repair.

When to replace a main shutoff valve

Old gate valves wear out. The rubber washer inside breaks down and the stem corrodes. Signs it is time to replace it:

  • The handle will not turn or feels like it might snap.
  • Water still flows with the valve closed.
  • The valve leaks at the stem or packing nut.
  • The pipe around it is rusty galvanized steel.

Replacing the valve with a full port ball valve usually costs $250 to $600 in the Burien area. It is a smart upgrade if you are already planning other work, such as a whole house repipe.

Teach everyone in the house

Show every adult and older child where the valve is. Hang a tag on it. Keep a flashlight and any tool you need nearby. If you have a crawl space valve, clear a path to it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I turn off the water at the meter myself?

You can in an emergency if the house valve fails, but the meter side belongs to your water district. You may need a meter key. Call your district first if you have time.

Why does water still trickle after I close the shutoff?

The washer inside an old gate valve may be worn, or the valve is not fully closed. A trickle means the valve is failing. Plan to replace it with a ball valve.

How long does it take to replace a main shutoff valve?

Most replacements take one to two hours. The water to the house is off during the work. Older galvanized pipe at the valve can add time if threads are badly rusted.

Should I shut off the water when I go on vacation?

Closing the main valve during a long trip is a simple way to prevent a flood. Turn the water heater to vacation or pilot mode first.
