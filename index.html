Plumbing Services in Burien, WA

Residential and commercial plumbing services in Burien cover everything from a leaking faucet to a full side sewer replacement. Most calls fall into a few groups: something is leaking, something is clogged, the water heater has stopped working, or the sewer line is backing up. When you call, you can ask for help with any of the services below. Each one has its own page explaining what the work involves, the warning signs, and what affects the cost.

Emergency plumbing

When a pipe bursts, a supply line fails or sewage backs up into a tub or floor drain, you need an emergency plumber in Burien who can stop the water fast. Providers handle burst and frozen pipes, overflowing toilets, failed shutoff valves and main line backups, then quote the permanent repair.

Drain cleaning

Professional drain cleaning clears clogged kitchen sinks, bathroom drains, tubs, showers, toilets, floor drains and main sewer lines. Local plumbers use drain snakes, motorized augers and hydro jetting, and many offer sewer camera inspection to find out why the same drain in your Burien home keeps clogging.

Water heater repair and replacement

No hot water, rusty water or a leaking tank? Providers repair gas and electric tank water heaters, replacing thermocouples, elements, thermostats, anode rods and relief valves. When a tank fails, they handle water heater replacement and tankless installation, including the seismic strapping and permit Washington requires.

Leak detection

A rising water bill, damp crawl space or the sound of running water usually means a hidden leak. Leak detection specialists use pressure testing, acoustic listening equipment and thermal imaging to pinpoint leaks behind walls, under floors and in the buried service line from the meter, then complete the leak repair.

Sewer line repair

Your side sewer carries wastewater from the house to the public main, and in Burien the property owner is usually responsible for it. Sewer line repair starts with a camera inspection, then fixes tree root intrusion, cracked clay pipe, sags and offset joints with spot repair, trenchless pipe bursting, lining or replacement.

Repiping

Older Burien homes may still have galvanized steel or polybutylene water lines that cause low pressure, rusty water and repeat pinhole leaks. Whole-house and partial repiping replaces that failing supply pipe with PEX or copper, usually over a few days, with pressure testing and inspection once the new plumbing is in.

Smaller jobs are welcome too. Providers commonly handle faucet and fixture repair, running or loose toilets, garbage disposal replacement, shutoff valve replacement, outdoor hose bibs, and sump pump problems. Mention the job when you call so you're routed to a provider who does that kind of work.

Emergency Plumber in Burien: Is It an Emergency?

A plumbing problem is an emergency when water or sewage is actively causing damage, or when you've lost a basic service like water or a working toilet. If that's you, call now and ask for emergency plumbing.

Call right away if

  • A pipe has burst or water is coming through a ceiling or wall
  • Sewage is backing up into a tub, shower or floor drain
  • The water heater is leaking heavily from the tank
  • You can't stop water from running, even at the fixture valve
  • Your only toilet is overflowing or unusable

If you smell gas near a gas water heater or appliance, leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not use switches or phones near the smell.

What to do while you wait

  1. Shut off the main water valve. It's usually where the water line enters the house, often in a crawl space, garage or utility area.
  2. Turn off the water heater: switch a gas unit to "off" or "pilot," or switch off the breaker for an electric unit.
  3. During a sewer backup, stop using sinks, toilets, the dishwasher and the washing machine.
  4. Move belongings out of the water and keep away from wet outlets and cords.
  5. Take photos of the damage for your insurance company.

Local Plumbing Contractors in Burien: How to Verify a Provider

The providers in our network are independent plumbing businesses that serve Burien and nearby communities. Their experience, credentials, pricing and warranties belong to them and vary from company to company, so it's worth confirming a few things directly with whoever you're connected to.

Washington requires plumbers to hold a plumber certificate from the Department of Labor & Industries (L&I), and plumbing contractors must be registered with L&I, which includes a bond and insurance. You can check both in a minute with L&I's Verify a Contractor, Tradesperson or Business lookup.

Questions to ask before work begins

  • What is your Washington contractor registration number?
  • Will the plumber doing the work hold a current L&I plumber certificate?
  • Do you charge a trip or diagnostic fee? Is pricing flat-rate or hourly?
  • Does this job need a permit, and will you pull it?
  • What warranty do you offer on parts and labor?

Larger jobs such as water heater replacement, repiping and sewer work usually need a permit. In Burien, plumbing permits are generally issued through Public Health – Seattle & King County, and side sewer work may also involve the local sewer district. A qualified provider should know which applies to your address.

Plumber Cost in Burien, WA: What Affects the Price?

Plumbing prices in Burien are set by each provider and depend on the job, so no fair price can be quoted before someone looks at the problem. These are the factors that move the price most:

  • Type of job and diagnosis time. A known fixture repair is quicker to price than a leak someone has to find first.
  • Urgency. Night, weekend and holiday calls often cost more than scheduled appointments.
  • Access. Tight crawl spaces, finished walls and ceilings, and steep yards add labor.
  • Materials and equipment. Tank or tankless water heaters, PEX or copper pipe, and camera or jetting equipment all change the cost.
  • Permits and inspections. Required for many larger jobs. Ask whether permit fees are in the quote.
  • Condition of existing plumbing. Corroded fittings or old pipe can turn a small repair into a bigger one.

Before a provider comes out, ask whether there's a trip or diagnostic fee, whether they price flat-rate or hourly, and whether you'll get an estimate in writing.
