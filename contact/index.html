Emergency

What should you do before the emergency plumber arrives?

Shut off water to the problem area, stop using drains that are backing up, and clear a path to the fixture, water heater, or crawl space hatch. Move pets and valuables away. Take photos of any damage for insurance. These steps limit damage and help the plumber start faster.

  1. Shut it off. Use the fixture valve under the sink or toilet, or the main valve.
  2. Stop drain use. No flushing, showers, dishwasher, or laundry during a backup.
  3. Clear access. Move items from under sinks and around the water heater.
  4. Find the crawl space hatch or cleanout. Many Burien homes have both.
  5. Protect belongings. Lift rugs and boxes off wet floors.
  6. Document. Take photos and short videos for your insurer.

If you are not sure where your main valve is, we will help you find it while you are on the phone.
