Trenchless sewer repair fixes a damaged side sewer through small access pits instead of a full trench. The two main methods are pipe lining, which forms a new jointless pipe inside the old one, and pipe bursting, which pulls a new pipe through while breaking the old pipe apart. Both avoid tearing up lawns, driveways and landscaping, which often saves money on restoration.

For many Burien homes, the side sewer runs under mature trees, rockeries, driveways or steep slopes. Digging a trench through all of that is costly and slow. Trenchless methods are often the better fit, especially on sloped lots in Seahurst and Three Tree Point where long side sewers run down a hill.

## Pipe lining (CIPP)

Cured in place pipe lining starts by cleaning the old pipe with a hydro jet. A flexible liner soaked in resin is pulled or inverted into the pipe and inflated against the walls. The resin hardens into a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one.

- Seals cracks and joints so roots cannot get in.
- Needs only one or two small access points.
- Slightly reduces the pipe's inside diameter.
- Works best when the old pipe is still round and in line.

## Pipe bursting

A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe by a cable. It breaks the old pipe outward and pulls a new HDPE pipe in behind it.

- Replaces the pipe with a brand new one.
- Can keep or increase the pipe size.
- Needs an entry pit and an exit pit.
- Works on cracked and broken pipe that is too damaged to line.

## Lining vs bursting compared

| Factor | Pipe lining | Pipe bursting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Result | New pipe inside old pipe | New pipe replaces old pipe |
| Access needed | Cleanout or one small pit | Two pits |
| Pipe diameter | Slightly smaller | Same or larger |
| Best for | Cracks, root joints, sound shape | Broken pipe, undersized pipe |
| Typical Burien area cost | $6,000 to $18,000 | $8,000 to $25,000 |

## When trenchless will not work

- **Bellies or sags.** Lining follows the existing shape, so a sag stays a sag.
- **Collapsed sections.** The liner cannot pass a total collapse.
- **Severe offsets.** Large shifts between sections block the liner.
- **Route changes.** If the line must move, digging is needed.

In these cases, a spot dig to fix the bad section, then lining the rest, can still save money. A spot repair alone costs about $2,500 to $7,500.

When there are driveways, patios, trees or slopes over the line, trenchless often costs less in total because restoration is minimal. For a short line under bare lawn, digging can be competitive. ## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a sewer liner last?
Quality cured in place liners are designed to last around 50 years when installed correctly.

### Do roots grow through a pipe liner?
No. The liner has no joints, so roots cannot get in. That is its main advantage over old clay pipe.

### How long does trenchless sewer repair take?
Most residential lining and bursting jobs take one day, plus time for permits and inspection.

### Do I need a permit for trenchless sewer repair in Burien?
Yes. Side sewer work requires a permit, and the sewer district usually inspects the connection.
