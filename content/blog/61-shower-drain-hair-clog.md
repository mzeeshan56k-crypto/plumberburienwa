A shower drain clogged with hair can usually be cleared by removing the drain cover and pulling the hair out with a plastic drain cleaning tool, a bent wire or needle nose pliers. If the clog is deeper, use a small hand snake. Avoid chemical cleaners, which rarely dissolve hair fully. If the tub and toilet are also slow, the clog is farther down the line.

Hair binds with soap scum and conditioner to form a dense mat just below the drain opening. Every shower adds more. Long hair, pets bathed in the shower and soap bars make it worse. In older Burien homes, tub and shower drains often connect to galvanized or cast iron pipe, which catches buildup faster than smooth plastic.

## Step by step: clear the clog

1. **Remove the drain cover.** Most unscrew with a screwdriver or lift out. Some twist off.
2. **Pull out visible hair.** Use gloves, a paper towel and needle nose pliers.
3. **Use a drain cleaning strip.** Push the plastic barbed tool into the drain, twist and pull. Repeat until it comes out clean.
4. **Flush with hot water.** Run hot water for a minute to carry away loose debris.
5. **Use a hand snake if needed.** Feed it into the drain, turn and pull back.
6. **Try a plunger.** Cover the overflow on a tub with a wet rag and plunge firmly.

## What not to do

- Do not pour chemical drain cleaner in a shower. It splashes, damages finishes and rarely clears hair. See [chemical drain cleaners](/blog/chemical-drain-cleaners/).
- Do not push the clog deeper with a wire hanger.
- Do not ignore slow draining, which leads to a full clog and standing water.

## When the shower clog is not just hair

| Sign | Likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| Only the shower is slow | Hair clog near the drain |
| Tub and bathroom sink both slow | Shared branch line clog |
| Shower backs up when the toilet flushes | Main line clog |
| Gurgling from the shower drain | Vent or main line issue |
| Sewer smell from the shower | Dry trap or drain buildup |

If the shower backs up when other fixtures drain, stop and read [gurgling drains](/blog/gurgling-drains/). A main line clog needs a plumber.

## Prevent hair clogs

- Use a mesh or silicone hair catcher.
- Brush long hair before showering.
- Clean the drain cover once a week.
- Use a drain cleaning strip once a month.
- Rinse the drain with hot water after each shower.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I unclog a shower drain without chemicals?
Remove the cover, pull out hair with a drain cleaning tool or pliers, then flush with hot water. A plunger or hand snake handles deeper clogs.

### Why does my shower drain clog so often?
Hair and soap scum build up quickly. A hair catcher and weekly cleaning prevent most repeat clogs.

### Can baking soda and vinegar clear a hair clog?
They can loosen soap scum, but they do not break down hair. Remove the hair mechanically.

### When should I call a plumber for a shower clog?
Call if the clog is past the trap, other drains are slow, or water backs up into the shower from other fixtures.
