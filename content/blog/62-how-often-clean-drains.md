How often to clean drains depends on the drain. Clean stoppers and hair catchers weekly, flush kitchen drains with hot water and treat them with an enzyme cleaner monthly, and clean P traps a few times a year. Most homes only need professional main line cleaning when problems appear, but homes with known roots in a clay side sewer benefit from cleaning every 12 to 18 months.

Clogs rarely happen all at once. Grease, hair and soap build up slowly until the drain finally stops. A simple routine keeps that buildup from reaching the point of a backup. It costs little, takes minutes and is especially useful in older Burien homes, where drain pipes are often galvanized or cast iron with rough inside walls.

## Drain cleaning schedule

| Drain | Task | How often |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bathroom sinks | Clean the pop up stopper | Monthly |
| Showers and tubs | Clean the hair catcher and cover | Weekly |
| Kitchen sink | Hot water flush, enzyme cleaner | Monthly |
| Kitchen sink | Clean the P trap | Two or three times a year |
| Garbage disposal | Grind ice and citrus peel | Monthly |
| Floor drains | Pour water to refill the trap | Monthly |
| Laundry drain | Check the lint trap or filter | Monthly |
| Main sewer line, no known issues | Professional check | When symptoms appear |
| Main sewer line with roots | Professional cleaning | Every 12 to 18 months |

## Simple monthly routine

1. Pour a kettle of very hot water down each kitchen and bathroom drain.
2. Add an enzyme drain cleaner to the kitchen sink at night.
3. Pull and clean bathroom sink stoppers.
4. Run water in rarely used drains to keep traps full.

## Signs you need professional drain cleaning

- More than one drain is slow.
- Drains gurgle.
- Water backs up into a tub or floor drain.
- Clogs return within weeks of clearing.
- A sewer smell does not go away.

These point to a clog in a shared line or the main sewer. Read [gurgling drains](/blog/gurgling-drains/) for what those sounds mean.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do drains need to be professionally cleaned every year?
Most homes do not. Professional cleaning makes sense when there are symptoms, or yearly if roots or heavy grease are known problems.

### Are monthly enzyme treatments worth it?
They help reduce light buildup in kitchen and bathroom drains and are safe for pipes. They do not clear active clogs.

### What is the best way to keep drains clear?
Strainers, hair catchers, keeping grease out and hot water flushes prevent most clogs.

### Can hydro jetting be done as preventive maintenance?
Yes. Homes with heavy kitchen use or known roots often jet every two to three years to prevent backups.
