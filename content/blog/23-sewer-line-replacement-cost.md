Sewer line replacement cost in Burien usually ranges from $8,000 to $25,000 for a full replacement by digging or pipe bursting. Trenchless lining costs about $6,000 to $18,000, and a spot repair of one section costs $2,500 to $7,500. Clearing roots without repairing the pipe costs $250 to $600. Length, depth, slope and what sits on top of the line drive the final price.

Many homes around Burien have side sewers from the 1940s to 1960s. Clay and concrete pipe from that era cracks, separates and fills with roots. Homes on sloped lots in Seahurst, Three Tree Point and along Marine View Drive SW often have long side sewers that run down a hill to the main. That makes some local jobs larger than national averages suggest.

## What drives the price

1. **Length of the line.** Longer runs mean more pipe and labor.
2. **Depth.** Deeper lines need more digging and shoring.
3. **Slope.** Steep lots add access and safety work.
4. **What is on top.** Driveways, patios, retaining walls and landscaping add restoration costs.
5. **Trees.** Large roots slow digging and may need cutting.
6. **Connection at the main.** Work in the street or right of way adds permits and traffic control.
7. **Pipe condition.** Collapsed sections rule out lining.

## Trenchless vs digging

Trenchless methods avoid a long open trench across your yard. Lining forms a new pipe inside the old one. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through while breaking the old one apart. Both need only small access pits. Digging is sometimes required when the pipe has collapsed, has a deep sag or needs to change route. ## Permits and the sewer district

Side sewer repairs need a permit, and the connection is usually inspected. Burien is served mainly by Southwest Suburban Sewer District, with Midway and Valley View serving some areas. Each has its own rules. Learn more in [Burien sewer districts](/blog/burien-sewer-districts/).

## Start with a camera

Never approve a large sewer repair without seeing the camera video. It shows where the damage is, how far from the cleanout and how deep. Ask for a locate so the problem spot is marked on the ground. Read [what a sewer scope shows](/blog/sewer-scope-inspection/).

## Ways to manage the cost

- Fix only the damaged section if the rest of the pipe is sound.
- Schedule root cleaning while you plan a larger repair.
- Ask whether lining is possible before digging under a driveway.
- Check if your homeowners policy has a service line endorsement.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does sewer line replacement take?
Trenchless lining often takes one day. A dig and replace job usually takes one to three days, plus time for inspection and restoration.

### Is trenchless cheaper than digging?
Not always for the pipe itself, but it often saves on restoring driveways, lawns and landscaping. That can make it the lower total cost.

### Who pays for the sewer line under the street?
In many cases the homeowner is responsible for the side sewer all the way to the main. Confirm with your sewer district.

### Can I finance a sewer repair?
Many plumbing companies offer payment options. Ask when you get your quote.
