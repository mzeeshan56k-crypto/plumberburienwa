A backwater valve is a one way valve installed in your main sewer line that lets wastewater flow out to the street and closes if sewage tries to flow back in. Burien homes most likely to need one have basements, daylight basements or floor drains that sit below the level of the street or the next upstream manhole. Installation usually costs $1,200 to $3,500 in the Burien area.

Sewer backups are expensive and unpleasant. During heavy storms or a clog in the public main, sewage can rise in the system and push back into the lowest drains of homes connected to it. A backwater valve stops that flow at the point where your line leaves the house. It does not prevent clogs in your own side sewer, but it protects you from problems downstream.

## How a backwater valve works

1. Wastewater flows from your home through the valve toward the street.
2. A flap inside the valve swings open to let it pass.
3. If water starts flowing backward, the flap closes and seals the line.
4. When the backflow ends, the flap opens again.

## Do you need one?

| Situation | Backwater valve? |
| --- | --- |
| Basement fixtures below street level | Often recommended or required |
| Daylight basement with a floor drain on a slope | Worth considering |
| Previous sewage backup from the city side | Strongly recommended |
| Home on a hill well above the street | Usually not needed |
| Crawl space home with no basement drains | Usually not needed |

Plumbing code requires backwater valves in some cases where fixtures sit below the next upstream manhole. A plumber can check the elevations for your home.

## Maintenance

- Open the access cover once or twice a year and check that the flap moves freely.
- Remove any debris caught in the valve.
- Never block the valve open.
- Have it checked after any backup.

## What it does not do

A backwater valve does not fix roots, grease or sags in your own side sewer. Those cause clogs that back up into your home from your own waste. When the valve is closed during a backflow event, water you send down the drain cannot leave either, so avoid using water until the event passes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will a backwater valve stop all sewer backups?
It stops backups coming from the public sewer. It does not stop backups caused by a clog in your own side sewer.

### Can I use water when the backwater valve is closed?
Avoid it. Water you send down the drain cannot leave and may back up inside.

### How long does a backwater valve last?
With basic maintenance, many last 20 years or more. Flaps and seals may need replacing over time.

### Does insurance give a discount for a backwater valve?
Some insurers offer discounts or require one for sewer backup coverage. Ask your agent.
