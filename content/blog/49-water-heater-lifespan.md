A standard tank water heater usually lasts 8 to 12 years in the Burien and Seattle area. Tankless water heaters often last 15 to 20 years, and heat pump water heaters around 10 to 15 years. Local water is generally soft, which helps tanks last, but sediment, high water pressure and skipped maintenance can shorten that life. Once a tank passes 10 years, plan for replacement.

Water heaters rarely fail on a convenient day. Most homeowners only think about theirs when there is no hot water or water on the garage floor. Knowing the age of your heater and the warning signs gives you time to shop, compare rebates and avoid an emergency replacement fee.

## Typical lifespan by type

| Type | Typical lifespan |
| --- | --- |
| Gas tank | 8 to 12 years |
| Electric tank | 10 to 13 years |
| Tankless | 15 to 20 years |
| Heat pump | 10 to 15 years |

## How to find your water heater's age

Look at the label on the side of the tank for the serial number. Most brands put the manufacturing date in it. Common patterns:

- A letter for the month and two digits for the year, such as A18 for January 2018.
- Four digits that show the year and week, such as 1806 for the sixth week of 2018.

If you cannot read it, search the brand name plus "serial number date code", or ask a plumber.

## Signs your water heater is near the end

1. It is over 10 years old.
2. Rusty water from the hot tap only.
3. Rumbling or popping from sediment.
4. Water pooling under the tank.
5. Hot water runs out faster than it used to.
6. Rust or corrosion on the tank or fittings.
7. Frequent repairs.

If you see water under the tank, read [water heater leaking from the bottom](/blog/water-heater-leaking-from-bottom/). For rusty water, see [brown or rusty tap water](/blog/brown-rusty-tap-water/).

## What shortens a water heater's life

- **Sediment buildup** from never flushing the tank.
- **A worn out anode rod** that no longer protects the steel.
- **High water pressure** over 80 psi.
- **No expansion tank** where one is needed.
- **Very high temperature settings.**
- **Poor installation** or a cramped space.

## How to make it last longer

- Flush the tank once a year. Our guide on [how to flush a water heater](/blog/flush-water-heater/) shows the steps.
- Have the anode rod checked every 3 to 5 years.
- Keep the temperature at about 120°F.
- Install an expansion tank if your home has a pressure reducing valve.
- Test the relief valve once a year.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a water heater last 20 years?
A tankless model can. A tank heater lasting 20 years is rare. Most tanks fail before 15 years.

### Should I replace a 15 year old water heater that still works?
Planning a replacement is wise. It is well past its expected life and could fail without warning.

### Does soft water make water heaters last longer?
Generally yes. Soft water leaves less mineral scale, though it can wear the anode rod faster.

### What is an anode rod?
A metal rod inside the tank that corrodes instead of the tank. Replacing it before it is used up can extend tank life.
