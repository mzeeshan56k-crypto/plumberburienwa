To thaw a frozen pipe safely, open the faucet it feeds, then warm the pipe slowly from the faucet end back toward the frozen section using a hair dryer, a heating pad or warm towels. Never use an open flame. Keep the faucet open until full flow returns, and check the pipe for leaks as it thaws.

Frozen pipes are common in Burien during short cold snaps. Crawl space lines, pipes in outside walls and garage lines freeze first. The ice itself is not the problem. The pressure that builds between the ice and the closed faucet is what splits the pipe. Opening the faucet relieves that pressure and gives melting water a place to go.

## Signs a pipe is frozen

- No water, or only a trickle, from one faucet.
- Frost on an exposed pipe.
- A toilet that will not refill.
- A slight bulge in the pipe.
- A cold spot along a wall where pipes run.

If only one fixture is affected, the freeze is in the line to that fixture. If the whole house has no water, the freeze may be near where the main line enters, or in the crawl space.

## Step by step: thaw a frozen pipe

1. **Open the faucet.** Open both hot and cold on the affected fixture.
2. **Find the frozen section.** Start with pipes in the coldest areas: crawl space, garage or the cabinet under a sink on an outside wall.
3. **Apply gentle heat.** Use a hair dryer, a space heater kept at a safe distance, an electric heating pad or towels soaked in hot water.
4. **Work from the faucet back.** Start near the open faucet and move toward the frozen area so water can escape as it melts.
5. **Keep heating until full pressure returns.** A trickle means ice is still in the line.
6. **Check for leaks.** Watch the pipe and the area below it for drips as water starts moving.
7. **Thaw other pipes.** If one pipe froze, others may be close. Check the rest.

## What not to use

| Do not use | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Propane torch or open flame | Fire risk and can boil water in the pipe |
| Kerosene or propane heaters in a crawl space | Fire and carbon monoxide risk |
| Boiling water poured on PVC | Can soften or crack plastic joints |
| Hammering the pipe | Can crack frozen copper or steel |

## When to call a plumber

- You cannot find or reach the frozen section.
- The pipe is inside a wall or ceiling.
- The pipe has a visible crack or bulge.
- Water starts leaking as it thaws.
- More than one line is frozen.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to thaw a frozen pipe?
With steady heat, an exposed pipe usually thaws in 30 minutes to an hour. Pipes inside walls can take longer and may need a professional.

### Will a frozen pipe thaw on its own?
It may thaw when temperatures rise, but it can split before then. Thawing it under control, with the faucet open, lowers the risk of a burst.

### Should I turn off the main water if a pipe is frozen?
If the pipe is not leaking, keep the water on so it can flow once it thaws. If you see a crack or leak, shut the main off right away.

### Can I use a space heater to thaw pipes?
Yes, with care. Keep it well away from anything flammable, never leave it unattended, and do not use fuel burning heaters in enclosed spaces.
