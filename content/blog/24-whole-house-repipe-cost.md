Whole house repipe cost in Burien is usually $8,000 to $16,000 with PEX and $12,000 to $20,000 with copper. A small home with easy crawl space access can come in around $8,000 to $13,000. A partial repipe of one area costs $1,500 to $5,000. The final price depends on the number of fixtures, the number of floors, access and how much wall has to be opened.

Repiping makes sense when the water lines are failing throughout the house, not just in one spot. In Burien, that usually means homes with original galvanized steel pipe from the 1940s to 1960s, or copper that has started to develop pinhole leaks. Once a home has had two or more leaks in a couple of years, patching section by section usually costs more over time.

## What affects the price

1. **Pipe material.** PEX costs less and installs faster. Copper costs more in material and labor. Compare them in [PEX vs copper pipes](/blog/pex-vs-copper-pipes/).
2. **Number of fixtures.** Each sink, toilet, tub and appliance needs a line.
3. **Number of stories.** Two story homes need more routing.
4. **Access.** A crawl space or unfinished basement makes routing easier. Slab homes and finished basements cost more.
5. **Wall repair.** Some quotes include drywall patching, others do not. Ask.
6. **Main line.** Replacing the yard service line at the same time adds cost but often makes sense.

## Signs you need a repipe

- Low water pressure throughout the house.
- Brown or rusty water, especially in the morning.
- Two or more leaks in the last two years.
- Original galvanized pipe in a home over 50 years old.
- Blue green stains on copper or pinhole leaks.
- Polybutylene pipe, which has a history of failure.

Read [galvanized pipes in older homes](/blog/galvanized-pipes-older-homes/) and [pinhole leaks in copper pipes](/blog/pinhole-leaks-copper-pipes/) for more on these signs.

## What happens during a repipe

1. The plumber maps the existing lines and plans new routes.
2. Small access holes are cut where needed.
3. New lines are run from the main to each fixture.
4. Old pipe is usually left in place and disconnected.
5. Water is restored each evening on most jobs.
6. The new system is pressure tested and inspected.
7. Access holes are patched.

Most repipes take two to five days. A permit is required.

## Is a repipe worth it?

A repipe ends repeat leaks, restores pressure and clears up rusty water. It can help with home insurance and resale, since inspectors often flag galvanized and polybutylene pipe. For many owners of older Burien homes, it is a once in a lifetime fix.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I live in my home during a repipe?
Yes. Most families stay at home. Water is shut off during work hours and restored in the evening on most days.

### Do I need a permit to repipe in Burien?
Yes. Repiping requires a plumbing permit and inspection. Your plumber should pull it.

### How much drywall needs to be removed?
Usually only small access holes, not full walls. Crawl space homes need the fewest openings.

### Will repiping raise my home value?
It removes a common inspection concern and can make the home easier to sell. Buyers and insurers often ask about pipe type in older homes.
