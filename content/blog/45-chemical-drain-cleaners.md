Chemical drain cleaners are not a safe long term choice for most pipes. The strong lye, acid or bleach formulas create heat and reactions that can damage older galvanized, cast iron and some plastic pipes, eat at rubber seals and harm garbage disposals. They also rarely clear grease or hair clogs completely. Mechanical methods like plungers, traps and drain snakes are safer and work better.

Many Burien homes still have galvanized steel or cast iron drain lines from the 1940s to 1960s. These pipes are already corroded inside. Pouring a caustic chemical into them speeds up that corrosion. When the chemical sits on top of a clog it cannot dissolve, it stays in the pipe for hours, which is exactly when damage happens.

## How chemical drain cleaners work

| Type | Main ingredient | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Caustic | Lye (sodium hydroxide) | Turns grease into soap, produces heat |
| Oxidizing | Bleach, peroxides | Breaks down organic material |
| Acid | Sulfuric or hydrochloric acid | Dissolves almost anything, very dangerous |
| Enzyme or bacterial | Natural enzymes | Slowly digests organic buildup |

## Risks to your pipes

- **Heat damage.** Reactions can soften PVC joints and crack old porcelain.
- **Corrosion.** Caustic and acid cleaners speed up rust in galvanized and cast iron pipe.
- **Seal damage.** Rubber gaskets and garbage disposal parts break down.
- **Trapped chemicals.** If the clog does not clear, the chemical sits in the pipe.
- **Danger to plumbers.** Chemicals in a clogged drain can splash on anyone who later opens the pipe.

## Risks to you

- Burns to skin and eyes.
- Toxic fumes, especially in small bathrooms.
- Dangerous reactions if two products are mixed.

Never mix drain cleaners, and never plunge a drain that has chemical cleaner in it.

## Why they often do not work

Most household clogs are hair, grease or solid objects. Chemicals may burn a small channel through the clog, but the rest of it stays. The drain slows down again within days. Main line clogs and roots are out of reach entirely.

## Safer ways to clear a drain

1. **Boiling or very hot water** for light grease in metal pipes.
2. **A plunger.** Cup plunger for sinks, flange plunger for toilets.
3. **Clean the stopper and trap.** See [slow bathroom sink drain](/blog/slow-bathroom-sink-drain/).
4. **A hand snake** for clogs past the trap.
5. **Enzyme cleaners** for monthly maintenance, not active clogs.
6. **Professional cleaning** for main lines and repeat clogs. Compare methods in [hydro jetting vs snaking](/blog/hydro-jetting-vs-snaking/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can chemical drain cleaners damage PVC pipes?
Heat from caustic reactions can soften PVC joints, especially if the cleaner sits on a clog for a long time.

### Are enzyme drain cleaners safe?
Yes. Enzyme and bacterial cleaners are safe for pipes and septic systems. They work slowly and are best for maintenance.

### Is baking soda and vinegar a safe alternative?
It is safe for pipes and can freshen drains. It will not clear a solid clog.

### What should I do if a chemical cleaner did not work?
Do not add more chemical or plunge. Ventilate the area and call a plumber, and tell them what product you used.
