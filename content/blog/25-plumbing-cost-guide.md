Plumbing costs in Burien range from about $150 for a simple drain clog to $20,000 or more for a whole house repipe or sewer line replacement. Most everyday repairs, like a leaking pipe, a clogged toilet or a water heater part, fall between $150 and $800. The price depends on the job, access, pipe material and whether the visit is during the day or after hours.

Plumbing prices feel hard to predict because every home is different. A pipe in an open crawl space is quick to reach. The same pipe behind tile in a finished bathroom is not. Older Burien homes also add steps, such as working with galvanized pipe or clay sewer lines. The ranges below reflect typical local jobs in 2026.

## What changes the price

- **Access.** Crawl spaces, finished walls and slabs.
- **Pipe material.** Galvanized and cast iron take more time.
- **Time of day.** After hours and holidays add a fee.
- **Permits.** Water heaters, repipes and sewer work need permits. See [plumbing permits in Burien](/blog/plumbing-permits-burien/).
- **Code updates.** Older installs may need upgrades to pass inspection.

## How to get a fair price

1. Describe the problem clearly on the phone and ask for a range.
2. Ask for a firm written price before work starts.
3. Compare quotes on the same scope of work.
4. Check that the plumber is certified and the company is registered with Washington L&I.
5. Ask about warranty on parts and labor.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do plumbing prices vary so much?
Every job differs in access, pipe material and time. A leak in an open crawl space may take an hour. The same leak behind tile may take a day.

### Do plumbers in Burien charge by the hour or by the job?
Many give a flat price for the job after seeing the problem. This protects you from open ended hourly bills.

### Is the cheapest quote the best choice?
Not always. Check what is included, such as permits, parts and warranty. A low quote that leaves out key items can cost more in the end.

### Are estimates free?
A phone price range should be free. Some companies charge a service call fee for an on site diagnosis, which may be credited toward the repair.
