A home plumbing maintenance checklist helps you catch small problems before they turn into floods, clogs or failed water heaters. For a Burien home, focus on four things: freeze protection in fall, drain and sewer care, a yearly water heater check, and regular leak checks under sinks and toilets. Most tasks take minutes and need no special tools.

Burien has a lot of older housing stock. Many homes date from the 1940s to 1960s and still have galvanized pipe, clay side sewers and crawl space plumbing. These systems work for decades with care, but they fail in predictable ways. Rain and mild winters shape the risks too. Roots chase moisture into sewer lines, and short cold snaps freeze pipes that are rarely tested by cold.

## Monthly checks

- Look under every sink for drips, stains or a musty smell.
- Run water in unused sinks, tubs and floor drains so the traps stay full.
- Check that toilets stop running after a flush.
- Watch your water bill for jumps you cannot explain.

## Every three months

- Clean faucet aerators and shower heads to remove grit.
- Test the main water shutoff valve so it does not seize.
- Check washing machine hoses for bulges or cracks.
- Pour a kettle of hot water down kitchen drains to move light grease.

## Seasonal checklist

| Season | Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| Fall | Disconnect hoses, drain hose bibs, insulate crawl space pipes, close crawl space vents, test the sump pump |
| Winter | Keep heat at 55°F or higher, open sink cabinets on outside walls during cold snaps, watch for frozen lines |
| Spring | Reopen crawl space vents, check hose bibs for split lines, look for leaks after the first outdoor use |
| Summer | Flush the water heater, schedule a sewer camera check if roots are a concern, check irrigation lines |

## Once a year

1. **Flush the water heater.** Draining a few gallons removes sediment and helps the tank last longer. Our guide on [how to flush a water heater](/blog/flush-water-heater/) walks through it.
2. **Test the temperature and pressure relief valve.** Lift the lever briefly. Water should flow and stop. If it drips afterward, it needs replacing.
3. **Check water pressure.** A simple gauge on a hose bib should read about 40 to 80 psi. Higher pressure strains pipes and fixtures.
4. **Inspect exposed pipes.** Look for rust, green crust on copper, or damp spots in the crawl space.
5. **Check caulk and grout** around tubs and showers so water does not get behind the walls.

## Every few years

- Replace rubber washer hoses and toilet supply lines with braided steel.
- Have the sewer line camera inspected if your home has a clay side sewer or large trees near the line.
- Replace a main shutoff valve that is stiff or leaks.

## Warning signs to act on right away

| Sign | Likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| Water bill jumped with no new use | Hidden leak or running toilet |
| More than one drain is slow | Main sewer line clog |
| Low pressure in the whole house | Old galvanized pipe or a failing pressure valve |
| Rusty or brown water | Corroding pipe or an aging water heater |
| Sewer smell indoors | Dry trap, vent problem or a cracked line |

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should a plumber inspect my home?
Most homes do fine with a plumbing check every one to two years. Older homes with galvanized pipe or a clay sewer line benefit from a yearly look, especially before buying or selling.

### What plumbing maintenance can I do myself?
You can clean aerators, check for leaks, flush the water heater, drain hose bibs and test the shutoff valve. Leave gas lines, sewer work and anything inside walls to a licensed plumber.

### How do I keep drains clear without chemicals?
Use drain strainers, scrape plates into the trash, keep grease out of the sink and flush drains with hot water. Avoid harsh chemical cleaners, which can damage older pipes.

### What is the most common plumbing failure in older Burien homes?
Root intrusion in clay sewer lines and leaks from aging galvanized supply pipe are the two most common. Both give warning signs, such as slow drains or low pressure, before they fail completely.
