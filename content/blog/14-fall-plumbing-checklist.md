A fall plumbing checklist for Burien homes should cover freeze protection, rain readiness and a quick check of the water heater and sewer line. Disconnect hoses and drain outdoor faucets, insulate crawl space pipes, test the sump pump, clean gutters and downspouts, and flush the water heater. Doing it in October saves you from emergency calls in December.

The Puget Sound region gets most of its rain from October through March. Our winters are usually mild, but cold snaps can drop temperatures into the low 20s for days. That mix of heavy rain and short freezes tests different parts of the plumbing. Rain tests drainage and sewer lines. Cold tests exposed pipes and outdoor faucets.

## Outdoor plumbing

1. **Disconnect and drain hoses.** Store them inside.
2. **Shut off and drain hose bibs.** Close the indoor shutoff and open the outdoor faucet to drain it. See [how to winterize outdoor faucets](/blog/winterize-outdoor-faucets/).
3. **Add hose bib covers.** Especially on faucets without an indoor shutoff.
4. **Blow out irrigation lines.** Shut off the system and clear the water.
5. **Clean gutters and downspouts.** Overflowing gutters dump water near the foundation and crawl space.
6. **Check downspout drains.** Make sure water flows away from the house, not into a sewer cleanout.

## Crawl space and garage

- Insulate exposed water pipes with foam sleeves.
- Close or block crawl space vents for the coldest months.
- Look for standing water, damp insulation or musty smells.
- Seal gaps where pipes pass through outside walls.
- Insulate water lines in an unheated garage.

Our guide to [crawl space plumbing problems](/blog/crawl-space-plumbing-problems/) explains what to look for under the house.

## Drainage and sewer

| Task | Why it matters in fall |
| --- | --- |
| Test the sump pump | Rain season fills sump pits fast |
| Pour water in floor drains | Keeps traps full and blocks sewer gas |
| Clear slow drains now | Heavy use during holidays turns slow drains into clogs |
| Camera the side sewer if roots are a known problem | Roots grow toward moisture in late summer and fall |

If you have a sump pump, read our [sump pump guide](/blog/sump-pump-guide/) for how to test it.

## Water heater

- Drain a few gallons from the tank to flush sediment.
- Test the temperature and pressure relief valve.
- Check for rust or water around the base.
- Set the temperature to about 120°F.

## Inside the house

- Check under sinks for slow drips.
- Test the main water shutoff so it turns freely.
- Replace cracked washing machine hoses.
- Run water in guest bathrooms you rarely use.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I start fall plumbing prep in Burien?
Aim for October. That gives you time before the first freeze, which can come in November in some years.

### Should I turn off my outdoor faucets in fall?
Yes. Close the indoor shutoff, open the outdoor faucet to drain it, and remove the hose. This prevents split lines inside the wall.

### Do I need to flush my water heater every fall?
Once a year is enough for most homes. Fall is a good time because the heater works hardest in winter.

### Why check the sump pump in fall?
Rain season fills sump pits quickly. Testing it before the first heavy storm makes sure it will run when you need it.
