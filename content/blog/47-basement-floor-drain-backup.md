A basement floor drain backing up is usually caused by a clog in the main sewer line or side sewer, often from roots, grease or a sag in older pipe. Because the floor drain is the lowest opening, water from every fixture in the house comes out there first. Stop using water right away, avoid contact with the water, and call a plumber to clear the main line.

Many Burien homes with basements or daylight basements have a floor drain near the laundry, furnace or water heater. It connects to the same main line that carries waste to the street. When the side sewer slows down, wastewater has nowhere to go but up through the lowest drain. During heavy rain, an overloaded side sewer can do the same thing.

## Common causes

| Cause | Clue |
| --- | --- |
| Roots in the side sewer | Repeat backups, large trees near the line |
| Grease and debris buildup | Backup after laundry or dishwasher use |
| Sag or belly in the pipe | Repeat clogs at the same spot |
| Collapsed or broken pipe | Backups get worse over time |
| Rain overload | Backups only during storms |
| Clogged floor drain itself | Only the floor drain is slow, nothing comes up |
| Sewer main surcharge | Rare, during extreme storms |

## What to do when it backs up

1. **Stop using water.** No flushing, showers, laundry or dishwasher.
2. **Keep people and pets away.** Sewage water carries bacteria.
3. **Do not plunge it.** You can push sewage farther into the room.
4. **Move belongings** away from the water.
5. **Call a plumber.** A main line clog needs a drain machine and usually a camera inspection.
6. **Document damage** for insurance.

## How plumbers fix it

The plumber clears the line through a cleanout, a toilet flange or the floor drain. A cable cuts through roots and debris. Hydro jetting cleans the full pipe wall. A camera inspection afterward shows why it clogged, so you can decide on a repair. Our guide to [hydro jetting vs snaking](/blog/hydro-jetting-vs-snaking/) compares the methods.

## Prevent the next backup

- Clear roots on a schedule if the camera shows them. See [tree roots in your sewer line](/blog/tree-roots-in-sewer-line/).
- Keep grease, wipes and paper towels out of all drains.
- Repair cracked or sagging sections of the side sewer.
- Add a backwater valve if your basement sits below street level. Read [backwater valves](/blog/backwater-valve/).
- Keep the floor drain trap full of water to block sewer gas.

## If it only happens when it rains

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a basement floor drain backup an emergency?
Yes, if sewage is coming up. Stop using water and call a plumber right away.

### Why does my floor drain back up when I do laundry?
The washer sends a lot of water at once. A partly blocked main line cannot carry it, so it rises through the lowest drain.

### Should I clean up sewage myself?
Small amounts can be cleaned with gloves, boots and disinfectant. Larger spills on carpet or drywall need professional cleanup.

### Does insurance cover sewer backups?
Many standard policies exclude them. A sewer backup endorsement may cover the damage. Ask your agent.
