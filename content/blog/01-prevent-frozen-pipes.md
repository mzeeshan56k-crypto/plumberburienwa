To prevent frozen pipes in Burien, protect the pipes that sit in cold spaces before the first hard freeze. That means crawl space lines, pipes in outside walls, garage lines and hose bibs. Keep the house at 55°F or warmer, even when you are away. Know where your main shutoff is in case a pipe still splits.

Burien winters are mild most of the time. That is exactly why cold snaps catch people off guard. A few nights in the low 20s can freeze an uninsulated pipe in a vented crawl space. Many homes here were built in the 1940s to 1960s, with plumbing that runs under the floor in open crawl spaces. Homes on the bluffs near Seahurst and Three Tree Point also take more wind off Puget Sound, which pulls heat from exposed pipes faster.

## Which pipes freeze first in a Burien home?

Pipes freeze where cold air reaches them and warm air does not. In local homes the usual trouble spots are easy to predict.

- **Crawl space supply lines.** Vented crawl spaces drop close to outdoor temperature. Bare copper or galvanized pipe here is the most common freeze point.
- **Hose bibs and outdoor faucets.** Water left in the faucet or the pipe behind it freezes and splits the line inside the wall.
- **Pipes in exterior walls.** Kitchen and bathroom sinks on outside walls often freeze first, especially in older homes with little insulation.
- **Garage and laundry lines.** Unheated garages hold cold air for days.
- **Water heater lines in the garage.** The cold supply line into the tank can freeze even when the tank stays warm.

## Steps to take before a cold snap

Most of this work takes an afternoon and costs little. Do it in fall, before the first freeze warning.

1. Insulate exposed pipes in the crawl space, garage and attic with foam pipe sleeves. Seal the seams with tape.
2. Close crawl space vents for the winter, or cover them with foam vent blocks. Open them again in spring to control moisture.
3. Disconnect garden hoses and drain them. A hose left on traps water in the faucet.
4. Shut off and drain outdoor faucets that have an inside shutoff valve. Leave the outside faucet open so trapped water can drain.
5. Add insulated covers to hose bibs that do not have a separate shutoff.
6. Seal gaps where pipes pass through outside walls and the rim joist. Cold air leaks are a major cause of frozen pipes.
7. Find and test your main water shutoff so you can close it fast.

## What to do on the coldest nights

When the forecast calls for temperatures in the low 20s or teens, a few habits make a big difference.

- Keep the thermostat at 55°F or higher, day and night.
- Open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls so room heat reaches the pipes.
- Let a faucet on an outside wall drip slowly. Moving water is much harder to freeze.
- Keep the garage door closed.
- Do not turn the heat off in an empty rental or a home you are selling.

## Signs a pipe is already frozen

You turn on a faucet and only a trickle comes out, or nothing at all. You may see frost on an exposed pipe, or a bulge in the pipe. Toilets may refill slowly. If you suspect a frozen pipe, keep the faucet open and warm the pipe gently. ## If a pipe bursts

Close the main shutoff right away. In most Burien homes it is in the crawl space near where the water line enters, in the garage, or at a valve near the water heater. Then open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain the line. ## Frequently asked questions

### What temperature do pipes freeze in Burien?
Pipes in unheated spaces can start to freeze when the outdoor temperature stays near 20°F for several hours. Wind and bare pipe speed this up. Pipes inside heated walls rarely freeze unless the heat is off.

### Should I drip faucets during a cold snap?
Yes, on the coldest nights. Let a faucet on an outside wall drip slowly, both hot and cold if it is a single handle. The small cost in water is much less than a burst pipe repair.

### Do I need to close my crawl space vents in winter?
Closing or blocking vents during freezing weather helps keep crawl space pipes warmer. Reopen them in spring so moisture can escape and the space stays dry.

### Is a frost free hose bib worth it?
For most Burien homes, yes. A frost free hose bib shuts the water off inside the warm wall, so the outdoor part drains. It costs a few hundred dollars once and removes one of the most common freeze points.
