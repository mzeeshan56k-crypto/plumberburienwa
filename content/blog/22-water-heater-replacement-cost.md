Water heater replacement cost in Burien is usually $1,900 to $3,800 for a standard 40 to 50 gallon gas or electric tank, installed. Tankless water heaters cost about $3,500 to $7,000 installed. Emergency replacement after hours runs about $2,200 to $4,200. The price should include the heater, removal of the old tank, code required parts like an expansion tank and seismic straps, and the permit.

Most homes in the Burien area replace a water heater every 8 to 12 years. When the old one fails, it often fails suddenly, with a leak or no hot water. Knowing the typical price ahead of time helps you compare quotes quickly. It also helps you decide whether to stick with the same type or upgrade.

## What the price should include

- The new water heater
- Removal and disposal of the old tank
- New supply connectors and shutoff valve if needed
- Seismic straps, required in Washington
- Expansion tank, often required with a pressure reducing valve
- Temperature and pressure relief valve and discharge pipe
- Gas connector and venting updates for gas models
- Permit and inspection

Ask each plumber for an itemized quote so you can compare the same scope.

## What raises the price

1. **Access.** A heater in a tight closet, attic or crawl space takes more work.
2. **Code upgrades.** Older installs may lack straps, a drain pan or a proper vent.
3. **Switching fuel type.** Going from electric to gas, or gas to a heat pump, adds gas line or electrical work.
4. **Larger tanks.** 75 gallon tanks and high efficiency models cost more.
5. **After hours.** Night and weekend installs add a fee.

If your heater is under 8 years old and only needs a part, repair is usually cheaper. If the tank leaks or it is over 10 years old, replacement makes more sense. ## Frequently asked questions

### How long does water heater replacement take?
A standard tank swap takes about two to four hours. Tankless and heat pump installs can take most of a day.

### Is a bigger water heater worth the cost?
Only if you regularly run out of hot water. A tank that is too large wastes energy keeping water hot you do not use.

### Do I need an expansion tank?
If your home has a pressure reducing valve or a check valve on the main line, code usually requires one. Your plumber will confirm.

### Can I install a water heater myself in Washington?
Homeowners can do some work on their own home with a permit, but gas connections and venting carry real safety risks. A licensed plumber is strongly recommended.
