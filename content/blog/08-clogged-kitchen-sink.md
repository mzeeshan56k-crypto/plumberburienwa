A clogged kitchen sink is usually caused by grease, food scraps and soap that build up in the drain line. Start with boiling water, then a sink plunger, then clean the P trap under the sink. If those fail or other drains back up too, the clog is deeper in the line and needs a drain snake or hydro jetting from a plumber.

Kitchen drains clog more than any other drain in the house. Cooking grease looks like liquid when it goes down the sink. As it cools in the pipe, it turns solid and catches everything that comes after it. In older Burien homes, kitchen lines are often galvanized steel or cast iron, and their rough inside walls catch grease even faster.

## Step by step: clear a kitchen sink clog

1. **Remove standing water.** Scoop it into a bucket so you can work.
2. **Pour boiling or very hot water.** Pour slowly into the drain. This can soften a light grease clog. Do not use boiling water on a PVC drain that is already full of standing water for long periods.
3. **Use a cup plunger.** Seal the other side of a double sink with a wet rag or stopper. Plunge firmly 15 to 20 times.
4. **Check the garbage disposal.** Turn off its power. Use the hex key in the bottom of the unit to free a jam. Press the reset button.
5. **Clean the P trap.** Place a bucket under it, loosen the slip nuts by hand and remove the curved pipe. Clear the debris and reinstall.
6. **Use a hand auger.** Feed a small drain snake into the wall pipe to reach clogs past the trap.

## Methods to avoid

- **Chemical drain cleaners.** They rarely clear grease clogs fully and can damage old pipes and garbage disposals. Our article on [chemical drain cleaners](/blog/chemical-drain-cleaners/) explains the risks.
- **Mixing products.** Never mix drain cleaners. The fumes can be dangerous.
- **Forcing a large auger.** Heavy machines can crack old cast iron or punch through a trap.

## When the clog is deeper than the sink

| Sign | What it usually means |
| --- | --- |
| Only the kitchen sink is slow | Clog in the kitchen branch line |
| The dishwasher backs up into the sink | Clog in the shared kitchen line |
| Kitchen and other drains are slow | Clog in the main line |
| Gurgling from other fixtures | Main line or venting issue |

If more than one drain is affected, stop plunging. Pushing more water can cause a backup in the lowest drain. Read [gurgling drains](/blog/gurgling-drains/) for more on what those sounds mean.

## How plumbers clear kitchen lines

- **Cable machine.** Cuts through the clog and pulls out debris.
- **Hydro jetting.** High pressure water scours grease off the pipe walls. It is the best fix for kitchen lines that clog again and again. Compare the two in [hydro jetting vs snaking](/blog/hydro-jetting-vs-snaking/).
- **Camera inspection.** Finds sags, breaks or heavy buildup in repeat problem lines.

## Prevent the next clog

- Wipe greasy pans with a paper towel before washing.
- Pour cooled grease into a can and toss it in the trash.
- Scrape plates into the trash or compost.
- Use a sink strainer.
- Run plenty of cold water when using the disposal.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does my kitchen sink keep clogging?
Grease and food scraps coat the inside of the pipe and narrow it over time. A cable clears a path, but hydro jetting removes the buildup so the clog does not return quickly.

### Can baking soda and vinegar unclog a kitchen sink?
It can help freshen a slow drain, but it rarely clears a real grease clog. Hot water and a plunger work better, followed by cleaning the P trap.

### Is it safe to use a plunger with a garbage disposal?
Yes. Turn the disposal off first, and cover the dishwasher drain hose connection or the other sink so the pressure goes into the clog.
