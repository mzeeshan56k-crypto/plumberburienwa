Water hammer is the loud bang or thud you hear when a faucet, washer or dishwasher shuts water off quickly. The moving water stops suddenly and sends a shock wave through the pipes. Common fixes include securing loose pipes, installing water hammer arrestors near quick closing valves, lowering high water pressure and restoring air chambers. Left alone, water hammer can loosen joints and cause leaks.

Many Burien homes have older pipes that run through crawl spaces with long straight runs and few supports. Those pipes are free to move and slap against framing. Homes with high street pressure and no working pressure reducing valve are also prone to water hammer. Modern appliances with fast closing solenoid valves, like washing machines and dishwashers, make it worse.

## Types of pipe noise

| Noise | Likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| Loud bang when a faucet or appliance shuts off | Water hammer |
| Rattling while water runs | Loose pipes |
| Ticking as hot water runs | Pipes expanding against framing |
| Whistling | Worn valve or high pressure |
| Humming | High pressure or a failing pressure reducing valve |
| Gurgling from drains | Drain or vent issue, see [gurgling drains](/blog/gurgling-drains/) |

## Common causes of water hammer

1. **High water pressure** above 80 psi.
2. **Quick closing valves** on washers, dishwashers and ice makers.
3. **Loose pipes** with missing or broken straps.
4. **Waterlogged air chambers** in older homes.
5. **A failing pressure reducing valve.**

## How to fix it

**Check your water pressure.** Attach a gauge to a hose bib. If it reads over 80 psi, the pressure reducing valve may need adjusting or replacing. See [pressure reducing valves](/blog/pressure-reducing-valve/).

**Secure loose pipes.** In the crawl space or basement, add pipe straps and cushioned clamps where pipes move.

**Install water hammer arrestors.** These small devices have a sealed air cushion that absorbs the shock. They screw onto washer valves or install near fixtures.

**Recharge air chambers.** Older homes may have vertical pipe stubs that act as air cushions. To recharge them, shut off the main, open the highest and lowest faucets to drain the system, then refill.

## Why it matters

Each bang stresses joints, valves and fixtures. Over time, water hammer can cause leaks at fittings, damage appliance valves and even split older pipe. If your home has aging copper, high pressure can also speed up [pinhole leaks](/blog/pinhole-leaks-copper-pipes/). ## Frequently asked questions

### Is water hammer dangerous?
It is not dangerous to people, but it can damage pipes, joints and appliances over time.

### Why did water hammer start suddenly?
A failing pressure reducing valve, a new appliance with fast closing valves or air chambers filling with water are common reasons.

### Can I install water hammer arrestors myself?
Screw on arrestors for washing machine valves are an easy DIY job. Arrestors inside walls need a plumber.

### Does water hammer happen in PEX pipes?
Less often. PEX flexes and absorbs some of the shock, but very high pressure can still cause noise.
