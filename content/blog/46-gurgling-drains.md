Gurgling drains mean air is being pulled or pushed through the water in a drain trap. The usual causes are a partial clog in the drain line, a blocked or undersized vent pipe, or a clog in the main sewer line. If one sink gurgles, look at that drain. If toilets gurgle when other fixtures drain, or several drains gurgle at once, the problem is in the main line or the vent.

Every drain needs air to flow smoothly. Vent pipes run up through the roof and let air into the system as water drains. When a drain or vent is partly blocked, the system pulls air from the nearest trap instead. That makes the gurgling sound and can siphon traps dry, which lets sewer gas into the house.

## What the gurgle is telling you

| What you hear | Likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| One sink gurgles as it drains | Partial clog in that drain |
| Toilet gurgles when the tub drains | Shared line clog or vent issue |
| Toilet bubbles when the washer drains | Main line clog or vent issue |
| Many drains gurgle at once | Main line or side sewer clog |
| Gurgling only during heavy rain | Overloaded side sewer |
| Gurgling plus sewer smell | Vent blockage or dry traps |

## Common causes

1. **Partial clog.** Hair, grease or soap slows the drain and traps air.
2. **Blocked vent.** Leaves, bird nests, debris or ice block the roof vent.
3. **Main line clog.** Roots, grease or a sag in the side sewer.
4. **Improper venting.** Remodels sometimes add fixtures without proper vents.
5. **Failed air admittance valve.** Some sinks use a small one way vent under the cabinet that can stick.

## What you can try

- Clean the stopper and trap of the gurgling sink. See [slow bathroom sink drain](/blog/slow-bathroom-sink-drain/).
- Plunge a single slow drain.
- Check the roof vent for visible debris, only if you can safely access the roof.
- Run water in all drains to see which ones gurgle.

## When to call a plumber

Call if:

- Toilets gurgle when other fixtures drain.
- More than one drain is slow.
- You notice a sewer smell.
- Water backs up into a tub or floor drain.
- Gurgling started after a remodel.

These usually point to the main line or the vent system. A plumber can clear the line with a cable or jet and use a camera to check for roots or sags. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is a gurgling toilet serious?
It can be. A toilet that gurgles when other fixtures drain often means a main line clog or vent problem, which can lead to a backup.

### Can a clogged vent cause gurgling?
Yes. Without enough air from the vent, the system pulls air through traps, which gurgle and can dry out.

### Why does my sink gurgle after the washing machine drains?
The washer sends a large volume of water at once. If the shared drain or vent is restricted, nearby traps gurgle.

### Can I fix gurgling drains myself?
A single gurgling sink often clears with trap cleaning. Main line or vent problems need a plumber.
