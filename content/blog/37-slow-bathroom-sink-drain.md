A slow bathroom sink drain is almost always caused by hair, soap scum and toothpaste caught on the pop up stopper or in the P trap under the sink. Pull out and clean the stopper first. If that does not fix it, clear the P trap or use a small drain snake. If more than one drain in the house is slow, the problem is farther down the line.

Bathroom sinks see a steady mix of hair, soap, shaving cream and toothpaste. That mix builds into a sticky clog just below the drain opening. In older homes, galvanized drain pipe behind the wall can also narrow with buildup. The good news is that most bathroom sink clogs are close to the surface and easy to reach.

## Step 1: Clean the pop up stopper

1. Look under the sink for the pivot rod attached to the back of the drain pipe.
2. Loosen the nut that holds the pivot rod and pull it out a little.
3. Lift the stopper out of the drain.
4. Clean off hair and gunk with a paper towel and an old toothbrush.
5. Reinstall the stopper and pivot rod.

Some newer stoppers twist or lift straight out without tools.

## Step 2: Use a drain cleaning tool

A plastic barbed drain cleaning strip, about $5, can pull hair out from below the stopper. Push it in, twist and pull. You will often bring up a large clump.

## Step 3: Clean the P trap

1. Place a bucket under the curved pipe.
2. Loosen the two slip nuts by hand or with pliers.
3. Remove the trap and clean it out.
4. Check the short pipe going into the wall for buildup.
5. Reinstall and test for leaks.

## Step 4: Use a small hand snake

If the clog is past the trap, feed a hand auger into the wall pipe. Turn the handle to break up the clog and pull it back.

## Do not forget the overflow

The small hole near the top of the sink is the overflow. It also lets air into the drain so water flows freely. If it is clogged with gunk, the sink drains slower. Clean it with a bottle brush and hot water.

## Which fix matches your cause?

| Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Hair on the stopper | Pull and clean the stopper |
| Soap and toothpaste buildup | Clean the trap, flush with hot water |
| Clogged overflow | Clean with a brush |
| Clog in the wall pipe | Hand snake |
| More than one slow drain | Main line or vent issue, call a plumber |

## Avoid harsh chemicals

Chemical drain cleaners can damage older pipes, chrome and stoppers, and they often leave the clog partly in place. Read [are chemical drain cleaners safe](/blog/chemical-drain-cleaners/) for the reasons.

## Prevent slow drains

- Use a hair catcher or a mesh strainer.
- Rinse the sink with hot water after shaving or brushing.
- Clean the stopper every few months.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is my bathroom sink draining slowly but not clogged?
A partial clog of hair and soap on the stopper or in the trap lets some water through. Cleaning the stopper usually fixes it.

### Does baking soda and vinegar work on a slow sink?
It can loosen light buildup and freshen the drain. It will not remove a solid hair clog.

### How do I remove a bathroom sink stopper?
Most pop up stoppers release when you loosen the pivot rod nut under the sink. Some newer types twist out by hand.

### When is a slow sink a sign of a bigger problem?
If the tub, toilet or other sinks are also slow, or you hear gurgling, the clog is in a shared line or the vent.
