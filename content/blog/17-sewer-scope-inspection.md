A sewer scope inspection uses a waterproof camera on a flexible cable to record the inside of your side sewer, from the house to the public main. In Burien, it is one of the most useful checks for older homes, because many side sewers are clay or concrete pipe from the 1940s to 1960s. The video shows roots, cracks, offset joints, sags and grease buildup.

Sewer problems are expensive because the pipe is buried. A camera removes the guesswork. It tells you whether a slow drain needs a simple cleaning or whether the pipe itself is failing. For home buyers, it can reveal a repair worth thousands before closing. For sellers, it answers questions before a buyer's inspector raises them.

## When to get a sewer scope

- You are buying a home built before the 1980s.
- More than one drain is slow or gurgles.
- You have had a main line clog in the last year.
- There are large trees between the house and the street.
- You are planning a remodel that adds a bathroom.
- Your home has never had the side sewer checked.

## What the camera can find

| Finding | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Light roots at one joint | Clear the roots, recheck in 12 to 18 months |
| Heavy roots at many joints | Lining or replacement |
| Offset joint | A section shifted, may catch debris |
| Crack or broken pipe | Spot repair or replacement |
| Sag or belly | Low spot that holds water and waste |
| Grease or scale buildup | Hydro jetting |
| Collapsed section | Replacement |
| Orangeburg pipe | Plan for replacement |

## How a sewer scope works

1. The plumber finds an access point, usually a cleanout. Our guide on [sewer cleanouts](/blog/sewer-cleanout-location/) explains where to look.
2. The camera is pushed through the line toward the main.
3. A screen shows the inside of the pipe live, and the video is recorded.
4. A locator tracks the camera head from above, so problem spots can be marked on the ground.
5. You get the video and a summary of findings.

If there is no cleanout, the camera may go in through a toilet flange or roof vent, which adds time.

## Reading the results

Ask the plumber to show you the video and point out each issue with its distance from the cleanout. Ask how urgent each item is. A single root intrusion is a maintenance item. A belly that holds water or a broken section is a repair item. ## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a sewer scope take?
Most take 30 to 60 minutes. Longer side sewers on sloped lots or lines without a cleanout can take more time.

### Is a sewer scope worth it for a newer home?
For homes built in the last 20 years with plastic pipe, problems are less common. It is still worth it if there are large trees or slow drains.

### Can a sewer scope damage my pipes?
No. The camera is pushed gently through the line. It does not cut or scrape the pipe.

### Do I get to keep the video?
You should. Ask for a copy of the recording. It is useful for repair quotes and for future buyers.
