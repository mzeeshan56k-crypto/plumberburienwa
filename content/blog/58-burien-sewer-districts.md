Burien sewer districts include Southwest Suburban Sewer District, Midway Sewer District and Valley View Sewer District, each serving different parts of the city and nearby areas. The district maintains the public sewer mains, usually in the street. The homeowner maintains the side sewer that runs from the house to the main. Your sewer bill or the City of Burien can tell you which district serves your address.

Burien grew from several communities, and utility service grew with them. Water and sewer service come from special districts rather than one city department. Southwest Suburban Sewer District, based on Ambaum Boulevard SW, serves a large share of Burien and nearby areas. Midway and Valley View serve other parts of the area. Boundaries do not always follow city lines, so neighbors a few blocks apart can have different districts.

## How to find your sewer district

1. Look at your sewer bill. The district name is on it.
2. Check the City of Burien's utilities page, which lists the water and sewer providers.
3. Call the City of Burien Permit Center if you are planning sewer work.
4. Ask your plumber, who can check the address.

## Who maintains what

| Part of the system | Who is usually responsible |
| --- | --- |
| Public sewer main in the street | Sewer district |
| Manholes and pump stations | Sewer district |
| Treatment of wastewater | Sewer district or King County, depending on the area |
| Side sewer from the house toward the main | Homeowner |
| Drains and pipes inside the house | Homeowner |

The exact point where homeowner responsibility ends can vary by district. Ask your district before planning a repair near the street.

## When to call the district vs a plumber

| Situation | Call |
| --- | --- |
| Many homes on the street backing up | Sewer district |
| Manhole overflowing | Sewer district |
| Only your home backing up | A plumber |
| Slow drains, roots, repeat clogs | A plumber |
| Side sewer repair or replacement | A plumber, who coordinates permits with the district |

## Permits and inspections

Side sewer repairs, replacements and new connections need a permit. The district usually inspects the connection to its main. A licensed plumber handles the permit as part of the job. Our article on [plumbing permits in Burien](/blog/plumbing-permits-burien/) covers which projects need permits.

## Water districts are separate

Your water comes from a different district. Burien is served by Highline Water District and King County Water Districts 20, 49 and 125. If you have a water leak at the meter, call your water district, not the sewer district.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the City of Burien run the sewer system?
No. Sewer service comes from special purpose sewer districts, mainly Southwest Suburban, Midway and Valley View.

### Who pays to fix a broken side sewer?
In most cases, the homeowner. The district maintains the public main.

### Can the sewer district clear a clog in my line?
Districts typically clear their mains, not private side sewers. If only your home is affected, call a plumber.

### How do I report a sewer overflow in the street?
Call your sewer district right away. Most have a 24 hour emergency line listed on their website and on your bill.
