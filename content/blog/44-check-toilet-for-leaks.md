To check a toilet for leaks, put 5 to 10 drops of food coloring in the tank and wait 15 minutes without flushing. If color shows up in the bowl, water is leaking past the flapper. Also listen for the tank refilling on its own, check whether water runs into the overflow tube, and look for water at the base of the toilet, which points to a failed wax ring.

Silent toilet leaks are one of the most common reasons for a high water bill in Burien homes. A worn flapper lets water trickle from the tank into the bowl all day. You may not hear it, but the tank keeps refilling. Over a month, that can add up to thousands of gallons.

## The food coloring test

1. Remove the tank lid.
2. Add 5 to 10 drops of food coloring or a dye tablet to the tank water.
3. Do not flush for 15 to 30 minutes.
4. Look in the bowl. Any color means a leaking flapper.
5. Flush afterward so the dye does not stain.

## Other signs of a toilet leak

| Sign | Likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| Color in the bowl after dye test | Worn or warped flapper |
| Tank refills on its own | Flapper leak |
| Water runs into the overflow tube | Fill valve set too high or failing |
| Constant hissing | Fill valve not shutting off |
| Water at the base after flushing | Failed wax ring |
| Water on the floor from the tank | Tank bolts or tank to bowl gasket |
| Drip from the supply line | Loose or worn supply line or valve |

## Fixing the most common leaks

**Flapper.** Turn off the water, flush to empty the tank, unhook the old flapper and install a new one that matches your flush valve size. Flappers are inexpensive and take minutes to replace.

**Fill valve.** If water runs into the overflow tube, lower the float. If it still runs or hisses, replace the fill valve.

**Wax ring.** A rocking toilet or water at the base means the seal under the toilet has failed. The toilet must be removed to replace the ring. This is a common plumber job because the toilet is heavy and must be reset level.

## Why it matters

A leaking flapper can waste dozens to hundreds of gallons a day, depending on the size of the leak. That shows up on your water bill. Read [why is my water bill so high](/blog/high-water-bill-causes/) for other causes.

A leak at the base is a different problem. It can soak the subfloor and the ceiling below. If you see a stain below a toilet, see [ceiling water stain](/blog/ceiling-water-stain-leak/). If the floor around the toilet feels soft, call a plumber soon.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much water does a leaking toilet waste?
A small flapper leak can waste dozens of gallons a day. A toilet that runs constantly can waste hundreds of gallons a day.

### Can a toilet leak without running?
Yes. A slow flapper leak lets water into the bowl quietly, and the tank only refills every so often.

### How long does a toilet flapper last?
About 3 to 5 years. Chlorine and minerals make the rubber stiff and warped over time.

### Is a leaking toilet base dangerous?
It is not dangerous to people, but water under the toilet can rot the floor and cause mold. Replace the wax ring soon.
