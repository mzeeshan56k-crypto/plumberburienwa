The basic garbage disposal dos and don'ts are simple. Do run cold water before, during and after use, feed food in small amounts and use it often. Don't put in grease, bones, fibrous scraps, potato peels, coffee grounds, pasta or rice. These either jam the blades or turn into paste that clogs the drain line past the disposal.

Many kitchen clogs in Burien homes start at the garbage disposal. The unit grinds food into small particles, then sends them down the same pipe as the sink. In older homes with narrow or rough drain lines, those particles settle and build up. A disposal used the right way causes few problems. A disposal used as a trash can causes many.

## What you can put in a garbage disposal

- Small, soft food scraps left on plates
- Most cooked vegetables in small amounts
- Soft fruit scraps
- Small amounts of citrus peel to freshen the unit
- Ice cubes, which help clean the grinding chamber

## What not to put in a garbage disposal

| Item | Problem |
| --- | --- |
| Grease, oil and fat | Hardens in the drain line |
| Bones | Jam the impellers |
| Potato peels | Form a starchy paste |
| Celery, corn husks, onion skins | Stringy fibers wrap around parts |
| Coffee grounds | Settle like sand in the pipe |
| Pasta, rice and bread | Swell and clump |
| Eggshells | Membrane wraps parts, shells add grit |
| Fruit pits | Can jam or crack the unit |
| Non food items | Damage the unit |

## How to use it the right way

1. Turn on cold water first.
2. Turn on the disposal.
3. Feed food slowly, a handful at a time.
4. Let it run until the grinding sound stops.
5. Turn it off, then run water for another 15 seconds.

Cold water keeps any fat solid so the blades can chop it. Hot water melts it so it coats the pipe instead.

## How to clean and freshen a disposal

- Grind a cup of ice cubes to knock off buildup.
- Grind a few small citrus peels for a fresh smell.
- Scrub the rubber splash guard with a brush and dish soap.
- Avoid bleach and harsh drain cleaners.

## How to fix a jammed disposal

1. Turn off the power at the switch, and unplug it or turn off the breaker.
2. Never put your hand in the disposal.
3. Insert the hex key, usually 1/4 inch, into the socket on the bottom of the unit.
4. Work it back and forth to free the jam.
5. Remove the object with tongs.
6. Press the red reset button on the bottom.
7. Restore power and test with cold water running.

## Repair or replace?

| Problem | Likely answer |
| --- | --- |
| Hums but will not spin | Jam, try the hex key |
| Will not turn on | Reset button or breaker |
| Leaks from the bottom of the unit | Replace the disposal |
| Leaks at the sink flange | Reseal the flange |
| Over 10 years old and noisy | Replace the disposal |

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I put coffee grounds in a garbage disposal?
No. Grounds do not clog the disposal, but they settle in the drain line and build up like sand. Throw them in the trash or compost.

### How long does a garbage disposal last?
Most last 8 to 15 years. Heavier models with stainless steel parts last longer.

### Should I use hot or cold water with a disposal?
Cold water. It keeps fats solid so they are chopped and carried away instead of coating the pipe.

### Why does my garbage disposal smell?
Food stuck under the splash guard and on the grinding chamber causes odors. Grind ice and citrus peel and scrub the splash guard to clear it.
