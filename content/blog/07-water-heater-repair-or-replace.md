Whether to repair or replace a water heater depends mostly on its age, whether the tank itself leaks, and how the repair cost compares to a new unit. Repair makes sense for a heater under 8 years old with a failed part, such as a thermostat, element or gas valve. Replace it if the tank leaks, it is over 10 to 12 years old, or the repair would cost more than half the price of a new heater.

Tank water heaters in the Burien area usually last 8 to 12 years. Our water here is generally soft, which is kind to tanks, but sediment still builds up and the anode rod wears out over time. Once the steel tank starts to rust through, no part can fix it. Many homeowners spend money on a repair only to replace the heater a few months later. A simple decision guide avoids that.

## How do you decide between repair and replacement?

| Factor | Lean toward repair | Lean toward replace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Age | Under 8 years | Over 10 years |
| Problem | A failed part | Leaking tank |
| Repair cost | Under 30% of a new heater | Over 50% of a new heater |
| Hot water supply | Enough for your family | Runs out often |
| Water color | Clear | Rusty from the hot tap only |

## Problems that are usually worth repairing

- **Electric heater with no hot water.** Often a failed heating element or thermostat.
- **Gas heater pilot will not stay lit.** Usually a thermocouple or flame sensor.
- **Leaking relief valve.** The temperature and pressure relief valve can be replaced.
- **Lukewarm water.** One failed element on an electric heater, or a thermostat issue.
- **Leaking drain valve.** A cheap part that can be swapped.

Our guide to [no hot water troubleshooting](/blog/no-hot-water-troubleshooting/) lists what to check before calling.

## Signs it is time to replace

1. Water leaking from the bottom of the tank, not from a fitting.
2. Rusty water from the hot side only.
3. Rumbling or popping from heavy sediment in an older tank.
4. The heater is past 10 to 12 years and needs a costly part.
5. Your household has outgrown it and runs out of hot water.

If you see water under the tank, read [water heater leaking from the bottom](/blog/water-heater-leaking-from-bottom/) for what to do right away.

## The 50 percent rule

If the repair costs more than half the price of a new heater, and the heater is past its midlife, replacement is usually the better value. A new heater also comes with a fresh warranty and better efficiency.

## Replacement options to consider

- **Same size tank.** The most common and affordable choice.
- **Tankless.** Endless hot water and less space, but a higher upfront cost and possible gas line upgrades.
- **Heat pump water heater.** Very efficient for electric homes, and Puget Sound Energy often offers rebates.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long do water heaters last in Burien?
Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with yearly maintenance.

### Is it worth repairing a 10 year old water heater?
Only for a cheap part on a tank that does not leak. For costly repairs on a 10 year old heater, replacement is usually the better choice.

### Can a leaking water heater tank be repaired?
No. Once the tank itself leaks, it must be replaced. Leaks from valves, fittings or the drain valve can often be fixed.

### Does a water heater replacement need a permit in Burien?
Yes. Water heater replacements generally require a permit and an inspection. A licensed plumber pulls the permit as part of the job.
