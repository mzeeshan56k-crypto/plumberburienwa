A sump pump collects water that drains into a pit, called a sump basin, and pumps it away from your home. Burien homes with basements, daylight basements or crawl spaces that collect water in the rainy season often need one. If you already have a sump pump, test it every fall, and replace it every 7 to 10 years or sooner if it struggles.

Burien gets most of its rain between October and April. Homes on slopes, such as daylight basement homes in Seahurst and along the bluffs, can see water moving downhill through the soil toward the foundation. Without a working sump pump, that water ends up in the basement or crawl space, where it damages flooring, rusts pipes and creates mold.

## How a sump pump works

1. Perimeter drains or the soil route water into the sump pit.
2. As the water rises, a float switch turns on the pump.
3. The pump pushes the water through a discharge pipe away from the house.
4. A check valve stops the water from flowing back into the pit.

## Signs your sump pump is failing

- It runs constantly, even in dry weather.
- It cycles on and off quickly.
- It makes grinding or rattling noises.
- The pit fills but the pump does not start.
- Visible rust or a stuck float.
- The pump is over 7 to 10 years old.

## How to test your sump pump

1. Make sure the pump is plugged in and the outlet has power.
2. Slowly pour a bucket of water into the pit.
3. Watch the float rise and the pump start.
4. Confirm the water drains quickly and the pump shuts off.
5. Go outside and check that water exits the discharge pipe away from the house.

Do this every fall, before the first big storm. It is one item on our [fall plumbing checklist](/blog/fall-plumbing-checklist/).

## Battery backup pumps

Big storms can knock out power right when the pump is needed most. A battery backup pump takes over if the main pump fails or the power goes out. For homes with finished basements, it is worth considering.

## Sump pump care tips

- Keep the pit clear of debris.
- Make sure the discharge line is not frozen or blocked in winter.
- Do not connect the sump discharge to the sanitary sewer unless your district allows it.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a sump pump last?
Most last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that run often in wet climates may wear out sooner.

### Why does my sump pump run all the time?
A high water table, a stuck float, a failed check valve or a pump that is too small can cause constant running.

### Can I install a sump pump myself?
Replacing a pump in an existing pit is a common DIY task. New pits, drains and discharge lines are better left to a professional.

### Where should a sump pump discharge?
Away from the foundation, to a spot where water drains away from the house. Check local rules before connecting to any storm or sewer line.
