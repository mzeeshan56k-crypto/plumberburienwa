Heavy rain affects your plumbing by overloading sewer lines, filling sump pits, flooding crawl spaces and pushing groundwater into cracked side sewers. In Burien, the most common rain related plumbing problems are backups in basement floor drains, slow drains during storms, sump pumps that fail and water collecting under the house. Most are preventable with a little fall preparation.

The Puget Sound region gets steady rain from October through April, with atmospheric river storms that drop a lot of water in a short time. Older homes with clay side sewers, roof drains tied into the sewer or no sump pump are most at risk. Sloped lots near the water, such as in Seahurst and along the bluffs, also see heavy runoff moving across yards.

## How rain causes plumbing problems

- **Groundwater in cracked sewer pipe.** Water seeps into broken clay joints, adding flow to the line.
- **Downspouts tied into the sewer.** Some older homes route roof water into the side sewer, which can overload it.
- **Crawl space flooding.** Poor grading and clogged gutters send water under the house.
- **Sump pump overload.** Pumps run constantly and can fail in a long storm.
- **Sewer main surcharges.** During extreme storms, the public system can back up into low drains.

## Warning signs during a storm

| Sign | Likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| Floor drain backs up during rain | Overloaded side sewer or main |
| Drains gurgle in storms only | Water in the sewer line |
| Sump pump runs nonstop | High groundwater or a failing pump |
| Musty smell after rain | Water in the crawl space |
| Toilets slow during storms | Partial blockage plus extra flow |

## How to protect your home

1. **Clean gutters and downspouts** before the rainy season.
2. **Extend downspouts** at least 5 to 10 feet from the foundation.
3. **Check grading** so the soil slopes away from the house.
4. **Test the sump pump** and consider a battery backup. Our [sump pump guide](/blog/sump-pump-guide/) covers testing.
5. **Camera the side sewer** if you have backups in wet weather. Cracks that let in groundwater also let in roots.
6. **Consider a backwater valve** if your lowest drain is below street level. Read [backwater valves](/blog/backwater-valve/).

## If a floor drain backs up during rain

Stop using water in the house. A backup in the lowest drain usually means the side sewer cannot keep up. Do not try to plunge a basement floor drain full of sewage. Call a plumber to clear the line and check for damage. Our guide to a [basement floor drain backing up](/blog/basement-floor-drain-backup/) explains the causes.

## Water in the crawl space

Most crawl space water after rain comes from runoff, not plumbing. Still, standing water rusts pipes, ruins insulation and can freeze around lines in a cold snap. Fix the drainage and dry the area. See [crawl space plumbing problems](/blog/crawl-space-plumbing-problems/) for what to look for.

## When to call for help

Call a plumber if sewage backs up into the house, a sump pump stops running during a storm, or drains slow down every time it rains. For storm related backups, call [Burien plumbing and drain help](/) at (833) 710-2588, any time of day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does my basement drain back up only when it rains?
Extra water is entering the side sewer, through cracks or connected downspouts, and the line cannot carry it all. A camera inspection shows where water is getting in.

### Can rain cause a sewer smell in the house?
Yes. Changes in pressure in the sewer during storms can push gas through dry traps or weak seals. Keep traps full by running water in unused drains.

### Do I need a sump pump in Burien?
Not every home needs one. Homes with basements or crawl spaces that collect water during the rainy season often do.

### Is it illegal to connect downspouts to the sewer?
Many sewer districts do not allow new storm connections to the sanitary sewer. Check with your district before changing any downspout connection.
