Signs of a hidden water leak

  • A water bill that's noticeably higher with no change in use
  • The sound of running water when nothing is on
  • Damp, warped or stained floors, walls or ceilings
  • A musty smell or mold growth, especially in a crawl space
  • A soggy or unusually green patch in the yard between the street and the house
  • Lower water pressure than usual
  • Cracks or bubbling in paint near plumbing walls

Check your water meter for a leak

  1. Turn off every faucet, fixture and appliance that uses water, and make sure toilets aren't refilling.
  2. Find your water meter, usually in a box near the street, and note the reading or watch the small leak indicator dial.
  3. Wait 15 to 30 minutes without using any water, then check again.
  4. If the reading moved or the indicator is spinning, water is leaking somewhere.

Next, shut the main valve at the house and check again. If the meter still moves, the leak is likely in the underground line between the meter and the house.

Where leaks hide in Burien homes

Many homes in Burien sit over crawl spaces, so a slow leak can run for months before anyone notices. When to call a professional

You can check the meter yourself and look under sinks and around toilets. Call a plumber if the meter shows a leak you can't find, you see water stains or mold, or you suspect a leak in the underground line. If water is actively coming through a ceiling, call for emergency plumbing first.

Leak detection FAQs

How do plumbers find a leak without breaking walls?

Plumbers combine pressure testing, acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging and moisture meters to narrow a leak down to a small area before opening anything. In most cases only a small access hole is needed right at the leak, which keeps drywall, tile and flooring repairs to a minimum.

Who is responsible for the water line between the meter and my house?

In most cases the property owner is responsible for the service line from the meter to the house, while the water district maintains the meter and the line on the street side. Check with the water district that serves your Burien address to confirm exactly where responsibility changes.

Can a hidden leak raise my sewer bill too?

It can. In many areas, sewer charges are calculated from water use, so a leak that runs through the meter may raise both bills. After the leak is repaired, ask your water and sewer providers whether they offer a leak adjustment and what paperwork they need.

Does homeowners insurance cover hidden leaks?

Coverage depends on your policy. Many policies cover sudden water damage, such as a burst pipe, but exclude gradual leaks and the cost of repairing the pipe itself. Read your policy or call your insurer, and keep the plumber's invoice and photos in case you file a claim.

How long does leak detection take?

Many leaks inside a home can be located in a single visit of a few hours. Underground service line leaks and leaks that only happen intermittently can take longer to pinpoint. The plumber can usually tell you after the first pressure test how complex the search is likely to be.
