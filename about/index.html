How can you check a plumber before you hire them in Washington?

In Washington you can look up any contractor on the Department of Labor & Industries "Verify a Contractor" tool. It shows registration, bond, insurance, and any violations. Plumbers working on your home also need a state plumber certificate. Ask for the contractor number on the call and check it.

We encourage every customer to do this, with us or with any plumber. It takes two minutes. Ask for the registration number, search it on the L&I website, and confirm the business name matches. This simple check protects you from unregistered contractors and helps if you ever need to file a claim against a bond.

Warning

Be wary of anyone who asks for full payment before starting a small repair.
