What plumbing repairs do Boulevard Park homes need most?

Boulevard Park's common problems are low water pressure and leaks from original galvanized pipe, root-packed clay side sewers, aging water heaters, and corroded shutoff valves that will not close in an emergency. Wet winters add yard drainage and crawl space moisture problems.

Low pressure and rusty water

Galvanized steel pipe was standard when many Boulevard Park homes were built. Over 70 years, rust builds up inside until water barely gets through. If your shower is weak and your hot water runs brown after sitting, the pipes are a likely cause.

Clay side sewers

Sewer lines from this era are often clay. Roots enter at the joints, and the line slowly fills. You may notice slow drains first, then gurgling, then a backup in the tub or a floor drain.

Old shutoff valves

Original gate valves often seize. When a pipe bursts, the valve will not close, and the only option is the meter box at the street. Replacing the main shutoff with a modern ball valve is a cheap upgrade that pays off in an emergency.

Crawl space moisture

Rainy months bring standing water to some crawl spaces. That speeds up corrosion on pipes and makes slow leaks harder to notice.

Prevention

How do you avoid frozen pipe repairs in Boulevard Park?

Insulate exposed pipes in the crawl space, close foundation vents, disconnect garden hoses, and cover outdoor faucets. On nights below about 20°F, let a faucet on an outside wall drip. If a pipe freezes, open the faucet, warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer, and never use an open flame.

Older Boulevard Park homes often have pipes that run close to the foundation vents with little or no insulation. Those are the first to freeze. If you find a split pipe, shut off the main valve right away. When the pipe thaws, it will leak. Call (833) 710-2588 for frozen pipe repair, day or night.
