What's included

Which plumbing services do Burien homeowners hire most?

Burien homeowners call most for emergencies, clogged drains, water heaters, hidden leaks, sewer line problems, and old pipes. Those six services cover almost every plumbing problem in a typical home. Each service below links to a full page with signs, process, and costs.

Emergency Plumber

Burst pipes, overflowing toilets, water through the ceiling, and sewage backups do not wait. Our emergency plumbing service runs 24 hours a day. We help you stop the water on the phone, then send a plumber to make the repair. You hear the after-hours fee before you book.

Drain Cleaning

Slow sinks, gurgling toilets, and tubs that fill with water while you shower all point to a clog. We clear kitchen, bath, laundry, and main sewer drains with cable machines and hydro jetting. If the clog keeps coming back, we run a camera to find out why.

Water Heater Repair and Replacement

No hot water, rusty water, popping sounds, or a puddle under the tank? We repair gas, electric, and tankless water heaters, and we replace tanks that are past their useful life. We help you pick the right size so you stop running out of hot water.

Leak Detection

A high water bill, a meter that spins with everything off, or a warm spot on the floor can mean a hidden leak. We find leaks inside walls, under floors, in crawl spaces, and in the yard line with as little cutting as possible.

Sewer Line Repair

Many Burien sewer lines are old clay pipe with roots in the joints. We inspect with a camera, clear roots, and repair or replace damaged sections. Where it fits, trenchless repair can save your yard, driveway, and landscaping.

Pipe Repair and Repiping

Galvanized pipe from the 1940s to 1960s rusts from the inside, which causes low pressure and pinhole leaks. We fix burst and frozen pipes, replace bad sections, and repipe whole homes with PEX or copper when patching no longer makes sense.

Good to know

How do you choose the right plumbing repair service?

Match what you notice to the most likely cause. One slow drain is usually a local clog. Several slow drains at once point to the main sewer line. Water where it should not be means a leak. If you are unsure, describe it on the phone and we will narrow it down.

What you noticeMost likely service
Water spraying, spreading, or coming through a ceilingEmergency plumber
One sink or tub drains slowlyDrain cleaning
Toilets gurgle and floor drain backs upSewer line repair
No hot water, or water under the tankWater heater repair
Water bill jumped with no new useLeak detection
Low pressure or brown water in an older homePipe repair or repiping

Pro tip

Text photos when we ask on the call. A picture of the water heater label or the leak helps us bring the right parts and finish in one visit.

Before you call

What should you ask before you hire a plumber in Burien?

Ask for the price before work starts, what is included, whether a permit is needed, and how long the repair should last. Also ask for the contractor registration number so you can check it with Washington L&I. A good plumber answers all of these without hesitation.

  1. Is there a trip or diagnostic fee? Know it before the truck rolls.
  2. Will I get a firm price before you start? The answer should be yes.
  3. Does the job need a permit? Water heaters and sewer lines often do.
  4. Is this a repair or a replacement, and why? You should understand the choice.
  5. What is your Washington contractor registration number? You can look it up on the L&I "Verify a Contractor" tool.
  6. What happens if the problem comes back? Ask what the plumber stands behind.

Warning

Be careful with big quotes that skip the inspection. A sewer or repipe price given without a camera or a look at the pipes is a guess.

Can you book a same-day plumber in Burien?

Yes, most repairs can be done the same day you call, as long as the plumber has the part on the truck. Emergencies get priority day and night. Larger jobs like sewer replacement or repiping are scheduled after the first visit because they need planning and sometimes a permit.
