The basics

What does water heater repair in Burien include?

Water heater repair means finding why the unit is not heating or is leaking, then replacing the failed part. Common fixes are thermostats, heating elements, gas valves, thermocouples, igniters, and relief valves. If the tank itself leaks, it cannot be repaired and needs replacement.

  • Diagnosis of no hot water, low hot water, or slow recovery
  • Electric element and thermostat replacement
  • Gas thermocouple, igniter, and gas control valve service
  • Temperature and pressure relief valve replacement
  • Flushing sediment and checking the anode rod
  • Tankless descaling, error code diagnosis, and part repair
  • Tank water heater replacement and haul-away
  • Seismic strapping and expansion tank install to code

Warning signs

What are the signs you need water heater repair?

The common signs are no hot water, hot water that runs out fast, rusty water, popping or rumbling sounds, and water around the base. A leak from the tank body means it is failing. A leak from a valve or fitting can usually be repaired.

  • No hot water at any tap
  • Hot water runs out much faster than it used to
  • Rusty or brown hot water only
  • Popping, banging, or rumbling while heating
  • Water pooling under or around the tank
  • Pilot light that will not stay lit
  • Tankless unit showing an error code

Pro tip

Check the label on your tank. The serial number usually shows the year it was made. Over 10 years old? Start planning a replacement.

How it works

How does a same-day water heater repair visit work?

The plumber checks power or gas supply, controls, and the tank. They test parts to find the one that failed and tell you if repair or replacement makes more sense. You get a firm price for each option, and work starts once you choose.

  1. Safety check. Confirm no gas smell and safe venting.
  2. Test parts. Thermostat, elements, gas valve, igniter, relief valve.
  3. Check the tank. Look for rust, leaks, and age from the serial number.
  4. Give options. Repair price and replacement price side by side.
  5. Fix and test. Make the repair, refill, and confirm hot water.

Pricing

How much does water heater repair or replacement cost in Burien?

Water heater repair in Burien usually costs $150 to $650. Replacing a standard tank water heater, installed, runs about $1,900 to $3,800. A tankless water heater costs about $3,500 to $7,000 installed. Permits, venting changes, and code upgrades can add to the total.

What changes the price

  • Gas or electric. Gas units cost more and need venting checks.
  • Size. A family of five may need a 50-gallon tank or more.
  • Location. Crawl space or attic installs take longer than garage installs.
  • Code items. Washington code requires seismic straps, and many homes also need an expansion tank.

Warning

If you smell gas near a gas water heater, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not try to relight the pilot.

Repair vs replace

Should you repair or replace your water heater?

Repair your water heater if it is under about 8 years old and a single part has failed. Replace it if it is over 10 years old, the tank is leaking, or repairs keep adding up. A new unit is also worth it if you keep running out of hot water.

Burien's water is soft, around 1 to 2 grains per gallon in published data. That means less mineral scale than many cities, so tanks here often last close to their full rated life. Age, not scale, is usually what ends a tank in Burien. Find the year in the serial number on the label.

Pricing

Is tankless water heater installation worth the cost?

A tankless water heater is worth it if you want endless hot water, plan to stay in the home for years, and have the gas line or electrical capacity to support it. It costs more up front than a tank, but it uses less energy and can last close to 20 years.

Soft water helps tankless units here, since scale is the main thing that wears them down in hard-water cities. The tradeoff is install cost. Many older Burien homes need a larger gas line or new venting. We tell you on the visit what your home would need.

Before you call

What should you ask before hiring water heater repair?

Ask whether the problem sounds like a part or the tank, what a repair and a replacement would each cost, and whether the plumber carries your model's common parts. If you are leaning toward replacement, ask about permit cost, haul-away, and code items like straps and an expansion tank.

  • Does this sound like a repair or a replacement?
  • What does each option usually cost for my size and fuel type?
  • Is the permit included in the price?
  • Will you remove the old tank?
  • Do I need an expansion tank or new straps?

Why act now

Why get your water heater fixed now?

A leaking water heater can dump 40 to 50 gallons onto your floor when it fails. A failing gas unit can also be a safety risk. If you see water under the tank, shut off the cold water valve and call. Fixing it early avoids flood damage and cold showers.

Frequently asked questions

Ask a Plumber Now
How long do water heaters last in Burien?

Tank water heaters usually last 8 to 12 years. Burien's soft water helps, so many reach the upper end. Tankless units can last 15 to 20 years with care.

Why do I have no hot water?

On electric units, check the breaker. On gas units, check the pilot light. If those are fine, a thermostat, element, or gas valve has likely failed. Call and we can walk through it.

Can you replace my water heater the same day?

Often, yes. Common 40 and 50 gallon tanks can usually be replaced the same day if we confirm size and fuel type on the phone.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Burien?

Yes, water heater replacement generally needs a permit. We include it in the quote and handle the paperwork.

Should I flush my water heater?

Flushing once a year removes sediment and helps the tank last. With Burien's soft water, sediment is lighter than in many areas, but it still builds up over time.

What size water heater do I need?

Most homes with 2 to 3 people do well with 40 gallons. Families of 4 or more often need 50 gallons or a tankless unit. We help you size it on the call.
