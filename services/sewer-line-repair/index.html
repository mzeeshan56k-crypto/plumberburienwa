The basics

What is sewer line repair and replacement in Burien?

Sewer line repair fixes the pipe that carries waste from your house to the public sewer main. Common repairs are clearing roots, replacing a broken or sagging section, lining the pipe from inside, or replacing the whole line. A camera inspection decides which one you need.

In most Burien homes, the homeowner owns the side sewer, which runs from the house to the connection at the street. When it fails, it is your repair. The good news is that most problems can be fixed without digging up the whole yard.

What is included in sewer line repair service?

Sewer service includes a camera inspection, line locating, root and blockage clearing, and a written repair plan with a firm price. If a repair is needed, we handle digging or trenchless work, permits, inspection, and restoring the area we opened.

Warning signs

What are the signs you need sewer line repair?

The main signs are several drains backing up at once, gurgling toilets, sewage smell indoors or in the yard, and soggy spots or lush green patches over the line. If water rises in a tub or floor drain when you run the washer, the main line is blocked or broken.

  • Two or more drains slow at the same time
  • Toilets gurgle or bubble when other fixtures drain
  • Sewage coming up in the lowest drain
  • Sewer smell in the house or yard
  • Wet, sunken, or extra-green patches over the line
  • Repeat clogs that come back within months

Repair vs replace

Why do Burien sewer lines need repair or replacement?

Many Burien sewer lines are clay or cast iron pipe from the 1940s to 1970s. Clay joints let roots in, and Burien's big trees send roots toward any water source. Cast iron rusts and cracks with age. Ground movement and long runs on sloped lots also cause sags and breaks.

  • Roots. Firs, maples, and cedars near the line push into joints.
  • Old clay pipe. Short sections with many joints.
  • Cast iron corrosion. Scale builds up, then the pipe cracks.
  • Slopes. Hillside lots in Seahurst, Three Tree Point, and Normandy Park have long, steep runs.
  • Wet soil. Saturated winter ground can shift poorly bedded pipe.

Warning

Root killer products only slow root growth. They do not fix the cracked joints that let roots in.

How do camera inspection and trenchless sewer repair work?

We clear the line so the camera can pass, record the inspection, and locate the problem. You see the video and get repair options with firm prices. Once you choose, we pull the permit, do the work, pass inspection, and restore the area.

  1. Clear and inspect. Camera the line from a cleanout or roof vent.

  5. Repair and test. Fix the line, flow test, and pass inspection.

What should you avoid to prevent costly sewer repairs?

Avoid grease, wipes, paper towels, and feminine products. Even "flushable" wipes do not break down fast enough and catch on roots in old clay pipe. Grease cools and sticks to pipe walls. Keeping these out of your drains is the cheapest way to make a sewer line last.

  • Cooking grease, oil, and fat
  • Wipes of any kind, even if labeled flushable
  • Paper towels and tissues
  • Coffee grounds and eggshells
  • Cat litter

Repair vs replace

Should you repair or replace your sewer line?

Repair one section if the camera shows a single break, sag, or root entry point and the rest of the pipe is sound. Replace or line the whole run if there are problems at many joints, the pipe is badly corroded, or it is collapsing. A full fix costs more once but ends repeat calls.

Ask a Plumber Now
Who is responsible for the sewer line in Burien?

In most cases the homeowner owns and maintains the side sewer from the house to the public main. Your local sewer district can confirm where your responsibility ends.

What is trenchless sewer repair?

Trenchless repair fixes the pipe without digging a long trench. Lining puts a new pipe inside the old one. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through as it breaks the old one. Both need only small access holes.

How long does sewer line repair take?

A spot repair often takes one day. Full replacement or lining usually takes 1 to 3 days, plus time for permits and inspection.

Can tree roots really break a sewer pipe?

Roots enter through small gaps at joints. As they grow, they widen cracks and can break or shift the pipe. Clay pipe is the most at risk.

Should I get a sewer inspection before buying a home in Burien?

Yes. Many Burien homes have older side sewers. A camera inspection costs a few hundred dollars and can reveal a repair worth thousands before you buy.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewer line repair?

Standard policies often do not cover wear, roots, or age. Some insurers offer a service line add-on. Check your policy and ask about sewer backup coverage too.
