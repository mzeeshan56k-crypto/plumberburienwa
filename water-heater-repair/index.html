What water heater services include

Water heater work covers diagnosis, repair of failed parts, routine maintenance, and full replacement or conversion. Common water heater services in Burien include:

  • No-hot-water diagnosis for gas and electric units

Signs your water heater needs service

  • No hot water, or hot water that runs out much faster than it used to
  • Rusty or discolored hot water
  • Rumbling, popping or crackling sounds while the tank heats
  • A rotten-egg smell from the hot water
  • Water around the base of the tank or dripping from the relief valve pipe
  • A gas pilot light that won't stay lit
  • Water that's too hot or keeps changing temperature

Repair or replace?

Replace if the tank itself is leaking. A leak from the tank body means the inner lining has corroded through, and that can't be repaired.

Repair if the problem is a failed part like an element, thermostat, thermocouple, gas valve or relief valve. These usually cost far less than a new heater.

Consider age. Many tank water heaters last roughly 8 to 12 years, depending on water quality and maintenance. If an older unit needs an expensive repair, replacement often makes more sense. The manufacture date is usually on the label.

Water heater codes and permits in Washington

In Washington, water heaters must be secured with seismic straps, and replacing one generally requires a permit and inspection. A proper installation also includes a correctly piped temperature and pressure relief valve, and many homes need a thermal expansion tank. Ask whether permit fees are included in the quote.

Water heater FAQs

How long does water heater replacement take?

A like-for-like tank replacement usually takes a few hours, including removing the old unit and installing seismic straps. Switching to a tankless model or moving the heater takes longer because gas lines, venting or electrical work may be needed. The plumber can give you a time estimate with the quote.

Why does my hot water smell like rotten eggs?

The smell usually comes from bacteria in the tank reacting with the magnesium anode rod and producing hydrogen sulfide gas. Replacing the anode with an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitizing the tank normally solves it. If only the hot water smells, the water heater is almost always the source.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

Replace it if the tank itself is leaking, since a corroded tank cannot be fixed. Otherwise, compare the repair cost with the unit's age. Many tank heaters last roughly 8 to 12 years. A failed element, thermostat or thermocouple on a younger unit is usually worth repairing instead.

Is a tankless water heater worth it?

A tankless heater gives continuous hot water and saves space, which suits larger households and long-term owners. The upfront cost is higher, especially if gas lines, venting or electrical service need upgrading. A plumber can compare tank and tankless options based on your home and hot water use.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Burien?

Generally yes. Water heater replacement in Washington usually requires a permit and inspection, and the new unit must be secured with seismic straps and have a properly piped relief valve. Your plumber should pull the permit and tell you whether its cost is included in the estimate.
