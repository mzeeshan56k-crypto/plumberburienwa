Plumbing services in White Center

White Center's housing includes many older single-family homes alongside duplexes, apartment buildings and storefronts, especially around the 16th Avenue SW business district. That mix means plumbing calls come from homeowners, landlords, tenants and business owners, each with different needs.

White Center is one of the communities covered by our local plumbing services in the Burien area. Here's how each service applies locally:

Drain cleaning

White Center plumbing calls range from clogged apartment kitchen sinks to restaurant grease lines along 16th Avenue SW. Grease and food waste harden inside pipes over time, and a basic snake only punches a hole through them. Hydro jetting scours the full pipe wall clean, which helps both homes and businesses avoid repeat clogs.

Sewer line repair

Many White Center homes and small multi-unit buildings are served by older side sewers that have been in the ground for decades. Cracks, roots and sagging sections lead to backups that can affect several units at once. A camera inspection shows the cause, and a plumber can recommend spot repair, lining or replacement.

Water heater repair

Landlords in White Center often need a water heater repaired or replaced quickly so tenants are not left without hot water. A local plumber can diagnose gas and electric tanks, replace failed parts, or install a new unit with seismic strapping and the required permit, and can also assess heaters across multiple units.

Leak detection

In White Center duplexes and apartment buildings, a hidden leak in one unit can damage the ceiling or walls of the unit below before anyone notices. Professional leak detection uses pressure testing, moisture meters and thermal imaging to find the source fast, which limits repair costs and disruption for neighbors.

Repiping

Older White Center houses and small apartment buildings may still have galvanized steel supply pipe that has corroded inside, causing low pressure, rusty water and repeated leaks. Repiping with PEX or copper replaces the failing lines and can be phased unit by unit in multi-unit buildings to keep tenants in place.

Emergency plumbing

A burst pipe or sewer backup in a White Center multi-unit building can affect several households at once, so speed matters. Shut off the water to the unit or building if you can, alert the landlord or property manager, and call to reach a local emergency plumber who serves White Center.

Plumbing for landlords and property managers in White Center

Rental properties make up a large share of White Center's housing, and plumbing is one of the most common maintenance issues landlords handle. A few habits reduce emergency calls and tenant complaints. Keep a record of each unit's water heater age and plan replacements before tanks start leaking. Label the shutoff valves for each unit and show tenants where they are. Replace old rubber washing machine and toilet supply hoses with braided lines during turnovers. If a building has had more than one main line backup, a sewer camera inspection will show whether the side sewer needs repair rather than repeated cleaning.

When a problem is reported, a quick response protects both the tenant and the property. Small drips and running toilets add to water bills, and slow leaks in multi-unit buildings often spread to neighboring units before they are visible.

What to expect when a plumber visits a White Center property

They will locate the relevant shutoffs, check the affected fixtures and explain the cause. You should receive a price before work begins, and for larger jobs a written estimate that notes any county permits and how long water will be off for each unit.

White Center plumbing FAQs

I'm a tenant in White Center. Should I call a plumber myself?

Usually your landlord or property manager is responsible for plumbing repairs, so contact them first and check your lease. In an emergency such as a burst pipe or sewage backup, shut off the water if you can, protect your belongings, and notify your landlord right away in writing.

Can you connect commercial properties with a plumber?

Yes. Restaurants, shops and other White Center businesses can call us. Tell us it's a commercial property and describe the issue, such as grease buildup or a backed-up floor drain, so you're connected with a plumber who handles commercial drain and sewer work.

Is White Center part of Seattle or Burien?

Neither. White Center is an unincorporated community in King County, located between Seattle and Burien. Because it isn't part of a city, permits for larger plumbing and sewer jobs go through county-level agencies. A plumber who works in the area regularly should know the process.

How often should a restaurant grease line be cleaned?

It depends on cooking volume, menu and whether the grease interceptor is maintained. Many busy kitchens schedule regular hydro jetting to prevent backups during service. A plumber can inspect the line with a camera and recommend a cleaning schedule that fits your business and protects the sewer.

What should landlords do about old water heaters?

Plan replacements before tanks fail. A leaking water heater can damage floors, walls and the unit below, and tenants without hot water need a fast fix. Keep a record of each unit's heater age, and have a plumber check older units for corrosion, leaks and proper seismic strapping.
